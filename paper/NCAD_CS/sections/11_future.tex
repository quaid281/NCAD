\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Beyond proposing a specific telemetry monitor, this work establishes a general framework for decomposing multivariate predictive errors into independently calibrated, correlation-aware alarm mechanisms. Replacing scalar residual norms with per-coordinate tripwires and an empirical healthy quorum (\emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} as the operational 128-dial detector; 224-dial \emph{Laminar-JEPA} as a diagnostic reference) yields four principal conclusions across 33 design-stage and 118 untouched archive streams:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Coordinate isolation and response:} Per-dial tripwires prevent drifting coordinates from inflating a shared threshold (Proposition~\ref{prop:hijacker_isolation}). Across the five-block archive, \emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} lifts live causal ER from $52.5\%$ to $64.6\%$ across all nine datasets ($p=0.0039$, positive in $12/12$ LODO folds), halves delay ($69.0$ vs.\ $133.5$ steps) on a $120\text{k}\text{--}129\text{k}$-parameter backbone ($93\text{--}141$ windows/s on one CPU thread), and doubles Point-F1 when $N_{\text{cal}}\ge 500$ ($0.040\to 0.085$).
    \item \emph{Label-free operating point:} At matched test FPR the panel does not outrank scalar scoring; its value lies in placing the label-free quorum at higher live coverage and shorter delay without test-label tuning.
    \item \emph{Concept drift and deployment gating:} While $88.1\%$ of untouched streams hold $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}\le 5\%$ (median $0.93\%$), post-calibration concept drift ($D_{\text{KS}}\ge 0.5$) rather than finite-sample quantile variance drives the $64.7\%$ $95$th-percentile tail, requiring $N_{\text{cal}}\ge 500$, held-out $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ gating, and live $D_{\text{KS}}$ recalibration.
    \item \emph{Open boundaries:} Isolated single-sensor hardware failures need dedicated low-FPR channel diagnostics, and slow ramps ($L\ge 4C$, including GHL) require a frozen-reference long-horizon drift monitor.
\end{enumerate}
