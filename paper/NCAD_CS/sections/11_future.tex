\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Operational deployment combines per-stream healthy-prefix fitting ($K\in[2,55]$ without pretrained cross-asset weights) with gated fallback routing rather than a distribution-free guarantee. Two-stage screening retains $52$ of $118$ streams (at $11.0\%$ mean prospective $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$), and adding $D_{\text{KS}}\le 0.50$ narrows acceptance to $46$ streams ($6.8\%$ mean test FPR), routing the remainder to 1D SPOT while holding live $D_{\text{KS}}\ge 0.50$ shifts in a $400$-window quarantine buffer until mode confirmation. Post-alarm LOCO masking provides synthetic perturbation triage rather than field localization.

Overall, our null regularizer results, matched-FPR ranking parity with scalar JEPAs, and high raw FPR for TimesNet and TranAD confirm that the panel's benefit comes from label-free operating-point placement rather than superior score discrimination. Operational deployment follows three fault-regime tiers: (1)~\emph{standalone fit} on abrupt or multi-coordinate contextual faults passing $N_{\text{cal}}\ge 500$, $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}\le 5\%$, and $D_{\text{KS}}\le 0.50$; (2)~\emph{complementary guards required} on $1/K$-diluted single-sensor flatlines, slow ramps, and uncalibrated regime switches; and (3)~\emph{1D SPOT fallback} on short prefixes ($N_{\text{cal}}<500$) or failed drift screens.

\vspace{-4pt}
\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Combining multi-horizon JEPA residuals, coordinate whitening, per-dial tripwires, and fallback routing across $33$ dev and $118$ untouched archive streams gives three conclusions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Operating-point shift and whitening:} Idealized per-dial equivariance motivates the quorum (approximate under additive floors), and Kronecker ZCA matches dense ZCA within confidence limits without claiming equivalence, while consistent PCA recovers causal shrinkage penalties. \emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} lifts causal ER over 1D JEPA ($52.5\%\to 64.6\%$, $p=0.0039$) and cuts segment-paired latency ($-41.4$ steps; stream-paired $-74.6$), while raising mean held-out ($6.4\%$) and test ($17.0\%$) FPR and leaving cross-dataset Point-F1 uncertain ($0.076\to 0.086$).
    \item \emph{Baselines and triage:} Matched-FPR checks show ranking parity with scalar JEPAs (1D leading F1 at $f=1\%$), whereas TimesNet and TranAD reach higher raw F1 only at $24.6\%\text{--}25.6\%$ healthy FPR. LOCO masking achieves $85.7\%$ Top-1 synthetic subset sensitivity on injected shifts.
    \item \emph{Prospective stability and quarantine:} Healthy-block permutation reduces measured archive FPR by $92.3\%$. Two-stage screening hits $11.0\%$ mean ($3.9\%$ median) prospective test FPR and $18.1$ false-alarm episodes per $1{,}000$ test steps ($n=52$); adding drift screening cuts test FPR to $6.8\%$ mean ($2.9\%$ median, $n=46$) alongside quarantined recalibration.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\emph{Future Work:} Key next steps include hardware root-cause validation, lightweight online $\mathbf{\Omega}_D$ updates, control-conditioned slow-ramp monitoring, and univariate flatline guards.
