\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We evaluated how Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures condition anomaly residuals under automated calibration across 33 primary and 141 archive-wide continuous telemetry streams:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Factorial Mechanism Isolation and Multi-Horizon Whitening:} Matched-encoder ablations separate score bounding from representation learning: LayerNorm bottlenecks compress residual kurtosis ($\gamma_2$ from $6.10$ to $0.91$), learned latent projections cut calibration drift in half ($D_{\text{KS}}$ from $0.376$ to $0.214$ in TS-JEPA), and multi-horizon barycenter whitening in Soliton-Reynolds JEPA lowers drift further to $0.179$ at $0.6\%$ causal FPR. On the fixed backbone, post-hoc Lieb--Thirring soliton modulation is statistically equivalent to the unmodulated multi-horizon envelope under TOST ($p_{\text{TOST}} < 10^{-4}$), and short horizons ($S \in \{1, 4\}$) eliminate the 63-step emission lag.
    \item \emph{224-Bit Multi-Perspective Circuit Breakers and Archive-Wide Generalization:} Latent scoring lowers held-out false alarms three- to four-fold under buffered attribution and ten- to forty-fold causally while doubling reconstructor event recall at a $1\%$ test FPR budget ($56.2\%$ vs.\ $21.7\%$ for TimesNet). Replacing 1D scalar score collapse and SPOT extrapolation with a 224-bit fluid-dynamic circuit-breaker panel and 32 fast right-edge onset relays eliminates hijacker-dial threshold inflation, more than doubling causal Point-F1 ($0.052 \to 0.138$ on $n{=}19$ streams, $p = 1.5{\times}10^{-5}$; $0.067\text{--}0.077 \to 0.139$ across all $141$ streams, $p = 1.2{\times}10^{-16}$), lifting causal event recall to $72.7\%$, and cutting detection delay in half.
    \item \emph{Calibration Drift and Adaptive Thresholding:} Static SPOT overshoots $q = 10^{-3}$ under buffered window smearing and by $15\times$ causally for single-horizon TS-JEPA, whereas barycenter-whitened Soliton-Reynolds JEPA reduces causal overshoot to $0.38\%$ stream-macro FPR across 33 streams and cuts the inter-block drift share four-fold ($71.3\%$ to $18.7\%$). Runs-declustered SPOT, per-dial hijack guards, and rolling quantiles narrow the remaining gap across step and variance shifts, motivating regime-conditioned conformal calibration.
\end{enumerate}
