\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Operational deployment pairs per-stream healthy-prefix fitting ($K\in[2,55]$, no cross-asset weights) with archive-informed fallback gates checked per fold in Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}(a). Two-stage screening retains $52$ streams ($11.0\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$) and drift screening ($D_{\text{KS}}\le 0.50$) retains $46$ ($6.8\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$), routing the rest to 1D SPOT while keeping live alarms active during passive $400$-window quarantine buffering.

Null regularizer results, matched-FPR ranking parity, and high raw FPR for TimesNet and TranAD show that the panel's benefit rests on automated label-free operating-point selection rather than better score discrimination (rescuing scalar under-triggering on stationary long histories, $49.8\%$ vs.\ $32.1\%$ ER across $46$ streams). Under cost weighting (Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}(c)), the routed hybrid is a recall-prioritized option ($w\le 5.88$), whereas false-alarm-averse monitors ($w\ge 6\text{--}20$) favor tighter gating or 1D SPOT. Deployment spans three tiers: (1)~\emph{standalone fit} on abrupt/contextual faults passing all gates; (2)~\emph{complementary guards required} on diluted flatlines, slow ramps, and regime switches; and (3)~\emph{1D SPOT fallback} when prefixes are short or drift screens fail.

\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Evaluations across $33$ dev and $118$ archive streams support three conclusions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Label-free operating-point selection:} Rather than better underlying discrimination, the quorum shifts the operating point (approximate under additive floors, Kronecker ZCA matching dense ZCA within CIs, and consistent PCA recovering causal drops). Unscreened \emph{Lite} lifts causal ER ($52.5\%\to 64.6\%$, $p_{\text{clus}}=0.0039$) and cuts segment-paired delay ($-41.4$ steps; stream-paired $-74.6$), while raising test FPR ($8.8\%\to 17.0\%$) with mixed dataset Point-F1 ($0.076\to 0.086$).
    \item \emph{Matched budgets and baselines:} Scalar JEPAs match ranking overall (1D leads F1 at $f=1\%$), whereas TimesNet and TranAD gain F1 only at $24.6\%\text{--}25.6\%$ FPR. LOCO masking hits $85.7\%$ Top-1 synthetic subset sensitivity.
    \item \emph{Recall-prioritized prospective trade-off:} Healthy-block permutation reduces archive FPR by $92.3\%$. Across all $118$ streams with fold-validated gates, three-stage routing targets recall-prioritized monitoring, trading $10.0\%$ vs.\ $8.8\%$ test FPR ($12.9$ vs.\ $8.8$ episodes per thousand steps) against 1D SPOT for $59.4\%$ vs.\ $52.5\%$ ER ($p_{\text{clus}}=0.0156$) and $0.091$ vs.\ $0.076$ F1, paired with mode-confirmed quarantine.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\emph{Future Work:} Next steps include hardware validation, online $\mathbf{\Omega}_D$ updates, control-conditioned ramp guards, and flatline monitors.
