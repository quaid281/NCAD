\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We investigated how Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures and fluid-dynamic multi-perspective circuit-breaker panels resolve both observation-space false-alarm flooding and 1D scalar score collapse across 33 primary and 141 archive-wide continuous telemetry streams:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{224-Bit Fluid-Dynamic Circuit Breakers Eliminate 1D Score Collapse:} Expanding $D=32$ multi-horizon whitened latent coordinates into $7 \times 32 = 224$ fluid-dynamic dials (128 sub-horizon flow positions, 32 Reynolds mean-flow transport dials, 32 turbulent-fluctuation dials, and 32 convective right-edge acceleration dials) with nominal-only hijack guards and 32 fast right-edge onset relays prevents isolated drifting coordinates from inflating scalar thresholds. This doubles strictly causal Point-F1 ($0.052 \to 0.138$ on $n{=}19$ primary streams, $17/0$ wins, $p = 1.5{\times}10^{-5}$; $0.067\text{--}0.077 \to 0.139$ across all $141$ streams, $114/18$ wins, $p = 1.2{\times}10^{-16}$), lifts causal event recall to $60.7\%\text{--}72.7\%$, and cuts detection delay in half ($246.6 \to 105.9$ steps on $n{=}19$ and $125.4 \to 55.3$ steps on $n{=}141$).
    \item \emph{Factorial Mechanism Isolation and Multi-Horizon Whitening:} Matched-encoder ablations separate score bounding from representation learning: LayerNorm bottlenecks compress residual kurtosis ($\gamma_2$ from $6.10$ to $0.91$), learned latent projections cut calibration drift in half ($D_{\text{KS}}$ from $0.376$ to $0.214$ in TS-JEPA), and multi-horizon barycenter whitening in Soliton-Reynolds JEPA lowers drift further to $0.179$ while cutting the inter-block drift share four-fold ($71.3\%$ to $18.7\%$). On the fixed backbone, post-hoc 1D Lieb--Thirring soliton modulation satisfies TOST equivalence ($p_{\text{TOST}} < 10^{-4}$), and short horizons ($S \in \{1, 4\}$) eliminate the 63-step emission lag.
    \item \emph{Contextual-Anomaly Dominance and Hybrid Quiet-Fault Coverage:} Soliton-Reynolds 224-Bit nearly triples reconstructor event recall at a $1\%$ test FPR budget ($58.4\%$ vs.\ $21.7\%$ for TimesNet) and leads on NASA contextual anomalies ($0.658$ buffered PR-AUC and $82.0\%\text{--}92.0\%$ matched-FPR recall), while a hybrid sensor-variance monitor recovers single-channel flatlines and dropouts ($41.7\%\text{--}50.0\%$ event recall), motivating online conformal circuit-breaker adaptation for non-stationary process ramps.
\end{enumerate}
