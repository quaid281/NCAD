\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We evaluated how Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures condition anomaly residuals under automated extreme-value calibration across 33 continuous telemetry streams under a chronological held-out protocol:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Factorial Mechanism Isolation and Multi-Horizon Barycenter Whitening:} Matched-encoder factorial ablations ($272\text{k}\text{--}312\text{k}$ online parameters) decouple score compression from representation learning: LayerNorm bottlenecks bound residual kurtosis ($\gamma_2: 6.10 \to 0.91$), while learned latent projections halve calibration-to-holdout drift ($D_{\text{KS}}: 0.376 \to 0.214$ in \texttt{TS-JEPA}) and multi-horizon barycenter whitening in \texttt{Soliton-Reynolds JEPA} further lowers drift to $D_{\text{KS}} = 0.163$ and causal held-out FPR to $0.3\%$. On the fixed multi-horizon backbone, one-sided Lieb--Thirring soliton concentration modulation trims buffered FPR from $12.2\%$ to $10.8\%$ while satisfying TOST equivalence in unadjusted Point-F1 ($\Delta = \pm 0.020$, $p_{\text{TOST}} = 0.026$), and short horizons ($S \in \{1, 4\}$) remove the 63-step emission lag.
    \item \textbf{Relative False-Alarm Control, Multi-Horizon Ranking Gains, and Hybrid Quiet-Fault Recovery:} Latent scoring lowers held-out false alarms and calibration error three-fold under buffered attribution and ten- to eighty-fold under causal streaming, while \texttt{Soliton-Reynolds JEPA} achieves leading buffered PR-AUC ($0.375$) and more than triples causal event recall at $1\%$ false-alarm budgets ($f^* = 0.01$: $0.701$ vs.\ $0.217$ for TimesNet). Pairing \texttt{TS-JEPA} with a rolling variance monitor raises quiet single-channel event recall from $0.19\text{--}0.28$ to $0.42\text{--}0.50$ at a $+15.1\%$ nominal FPR cost.
    \item \textbf{Calibration Drift and Adaptive Thresholding:} Static SPOT overshoots its $0.1\%$ design risk by $15\text{--}63\times$ out of sample under buffered attribution and $15\times$ causally for single-horizon \texttt{TS-JEPA}, whereas barycenter-whitened \texttt{Soliton-Reynolds JEPA} reduces causal static overshoot to $3\times$ ($0.25\%$ stream-macro FPR across 33 streams) and cuts the inter-block drift share from $56.6\%$ to $11.2\%$. Runs-declustered SPOT and rolling quantiles further narrow this gap across step and variance shifts, motivating future regime-conditioned conformal calibration.
\end{enumerate}
