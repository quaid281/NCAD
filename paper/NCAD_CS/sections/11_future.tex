\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We studied how Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures condition anomaly residuals under automated extreme-value calibration across 33 telemetry streams under a strict held-out protocol:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=0pt]
    \item \textbf{Mechanism Isolation and TOST Bounds:} Contrasting 12 backbones decouples score compression from representation learning: LayerNorm bottlenecks bound kurtosis ($\gamma_2: 6.10 \to 0.91$) and halve FPR, while learned latent projection halves calibration-to-holdout drift ($0.376 \to 0.209\text{--}0.214$) and lowers causal FPR to $0.009\text{--}0.028$. On a fixed Koopman backbone, the spectral regularizer satisfies TOST equivalence down to $\Delta = \pm 0.015$ Point-F1 ($p_{\text{TOST}} = 0.0024$) and $\pm 0.010$ ($p_{\text{TOST}} = 0.0088$; Reynolds within $\pm 0.035$, $p_{\text{TOST}} = 0.0031$), acting as an operator conditioning mechanism while the OpEntropy transition architecture cuts buffered delay from $110.3$ to $79.5$ steps ($127.0 \to 58.2$ causal).
    \item \textbf{Relative False-Alarm Control vs.\ Detection:} Latent scoring reduces held-out false alarms $3\text{--}4\times$ buffered ($7.4\%\text{--}10.1\%$ vs.\ $30.0\%\text{--}30.7\%$) and $8\text{--}20\times$ causally ($1.1\%\text{--}2.8\%$ vs.\ $22.2\%\text{--}24.3\%$) and raises low-FPR causal event recall ($0.371\text{--}0.431$ vs.\ $0.149\text{--}0.217$ at $f^*{=}0.01$), while Point-F1 and PR-AUC match reconstructors.
    \item \textbf{Calibration Gap, Refitted Permutation, and Boundaries:} Static SPOT misses its $q{=}0.1\%$ target out of sample ($1.51\%\text{--}1.95\%$ causal and $5.54\%\text{--}6.38\%$ buffered FPR for JEPAs, or $15\text{--}64\times$ overshoot); refitted permutations estimate that inter-block shift accounts for $56.6\%\text{--}64.1\%$ and serial dependence for $29.1\%\text{--}35.3\%$ of excess causal risk. Closing this gap requires declustered GPD fitting or online conformal updates, while $S{=}1$ eliminates the $63$-step emission lag ($S{=}64$) and quiet flatlines ($0.02\text{--}0.07$ recall) remain open limits.
\end{enumerate}
