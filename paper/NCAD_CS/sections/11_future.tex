\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We evaluated how Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures condition anomaly residuals under automated extreme-value calibration across 33 continuous telemetry streams under a chronological held-out protocol:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \textbf{Factorial Mechanism Isolation and Multi-Horizon Whitening:} Matched-encoder ablations ($272\text{k}\text{--}312\text{k}$ online parameters) decouple score compression from representation learning. LayerNorm bottlenecks bound residual kurtosis ($\gamma_2: 6.10 \to 0.91$), learned latent projections halve calibration-to-holdout drift ($D_{\text{KS}}: 0.376 \to 0.214$ in TS-JEPA), and multi-horizon barycenter whitening in Soliton-Reynolds JEPA lowers drift further to $D_{\text{KS}} = 0.163$ with $0.3\%$ causal FPR. On the fixed backbone, post-hoc Lieb--Thirring soliton modulation is a null modification satisfying TOST equivalence ($\Delta = \pm 0.020$, $p_{\text{TOST}} = 0.026$, Wilcoxon $p = 0.327$), and short horizons ($S \in \{1, 4\}$) remove the 63-step emission lag.
    \item \textbf{Relative False-Alarm Control, Threshold Trade-offs, and Hybrid Quiet-Fault Recovery:} Latent scoring lowers held-out false alarms three-fold under buffered attribution and ten- to eighty-fold causally. Soliton-Reynolds JEPA achieves leading buffered PR-AUC ($0.375$) and causal matched-FPR event recall ($f^* = 0.01$: $0.701$ vs.\ $0.217$ for TimesNet), trading lower causal SPOT Point-F1 ($0.060$ at a $2.2\%$ test alert rate vs.\ $0.161$ at $10.8\%$ for TS-JEPA) for $0.3\%$ held-out FPR. Pairing TS-JEPA with a rolling variance monitor raises quiet single-channel event recall from $0.19\text{--}0.28$ to $0.42\text{--}0.50$ at a $+15.1\%$ nominal FPR cost.
    \item \textbf{Calibration Drift and Adaptive Thresholding:} Static SPOT overshoots $q = 10^{-3}$ by $55\text{--}126\times$ under $S=64$ buffered window smearing and $15\times$ causally for TS-JEPA. In contrast, barycenter-whitened Soliton-Reynolds JEPA reduces causal static overshoot to $3\times$ ($0.25\%$ stream-macro FPR across 33 streams; $\text{FPR}_{\text{pt}} = 0.0025$) and eliminates inter-block drift ($71.3\% \to 0.0\%$). Runs-declustered SPOT and rolling quantiles narrow this gap across step and variance shifts, motivating regime-conditioned conformal calibration.
\end{enumerate}
