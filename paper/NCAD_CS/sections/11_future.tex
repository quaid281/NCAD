\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We evaluated how Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures condition anomaly residuals under automated extreme-value calibration across 33 continuous telemetry streams under a chronological held-out protocol:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Factorial Mechanism Isolation and Multi-Horizon Whitening:} Matched-encoder ablations separate score bounding from representation learning: LayerNorm bottlenecks compress residual kurtosis ($\gamma_2$ from $6.10$ to $0.91$), learned latent projections cut calibration drift in half ($D_{\text{KS}}$ from $0.376$ to $0.214$ in TS-JEPA), and multi-horizon barycenter whitening in Soliton-Reynolds JEPA lowers drift further to $0.179$ at $0.6\%$ causal FPR. On the fixed backbone, post-hoc Lieb--Thirring soliton modulation is statistically equivalent to the unmodulated multi-horizon envelope under TOST ($p_{\text{TOST}} < 10^{-4}$), and short horizons ($S \in \{1, 4\}$) eliminate the 63-step emission lag.
    \item \emph{Relative False-Alarm Control and Hybrid Quiet-Fault Recovery:} Latent scoring lowers held-out false alarms three- to four-fold under buffered attribution and ten- to forty-fold causally. Soliton-Reynolds JEPA matches TS-JEPA on buffered PR-AUC ($0.331$) and more than doubles reconstructor event recall at a $1\%$ test FPR budget ($56.2\%$ vs.\ $21.7\%$ for TimesNet), trading lower causal SPOT Point-F1 ($0.050$ vs.\ $0.161$ for TS-JEPA) for $0.6\%$ held-out FPR, while a hybrid variance monitor recovers quiet single-channel faults ($41.7\%\text{--}50.0\%$ event recall).
    \item \emph{Calibration Drift and Adaptive Thresholding:} Static SPOT overshoots $q = 10^{-3}$ under buffered window smearing and by $15\times$ causally for single-horizon TS-JEPA, whereas barycenter-whitened Soliton-Reynolds JEPA reduces causal overshoot to $0.38\%$ stream-macro FPR across 33 streams and cuts the inter-block drift share four-fold ($71.3\%$ to $18.7\%$). Runs-declustered SPOT and rolling quantiles narrow the remaining gap across step and variance shifts, motivating regime-conditioned conformal calibration.
\end{enumerate}
