\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We evaluated \emph{Laminar-JEPA} and its autopsy-distilled counterpart \emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} across 33 development and 118 untouched held-out telemetry streams (stream-level means unless noted):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Flagship vs.\ distilled circuit-breaker panels:} On the 33-stream development benchmark, \emph{Laminar-JEPA} and \emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} reach buffered Point-F1 of $0.244$ and $0.242$ and causal ER of $67.3\%$ and $65.0\%$ at $1.3\%$ and $1.0\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$, with \emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} cutting parameters by $35\%\text{--}52\%$, accelerating inference by $2.43\times$, and leading $7$-dataset macro Point-F1 ($0.171$). On the 19-stream residual cache, the 224-dial panel lifts causal Point-F1 over 1D collapse ($0.013\to 0.071$, $p=0.0023$), whereas the 160-dial subset ($0.098$) and training-whitened $\ell_2$ split-conformal rule ($0.072$) match or exceed it at lower complexity. Across $118$ untouched held-out streams under strictly disjoint checkpoint selection, \emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} lifts causal Point-F1 from $0.074$ to $0.085$ ($p=0.0034$), raises causal ER from $49.5\%$ to $67.0\%$ across $8/9$ datasets (sign-flip $p=0.012$), and remains positive in all $12/12$ LODO folds on the $141$-stream archive, while nominal quenching lowers four-horizon causal $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ across $7/9$ datasets ($5.57\%\to 5.37\%$, median $0.96\%$, $p=0.016$).
    \item \emph{Mechanisms and horizon latency:} Matched-backbone controls measure LayerNorm tail compression and barycenter drift reduction, while scalar $R_{\text{LT}}$ modulation is null. On five exploratory streams ($S\in\{1,4,16,32,64\}$), both panels retain a $2.1\times\text{--}4.3\times$ stream-mean causal Point-F1 gain over 1D scoring down to zero reporting lag ($S=1$), without establishing cross-domain latency rules.
    \item \emph{Calibration, single-sensor, and slow-ramp boundaries:} Stream-specific Sokal autocorrelation ($\tau_{\text{int}}$ up to $206$) leaves expected tail counts of $1.5$ ($q_{0.995}$) and $0.3$ ($q_{0.999}$) at $N_{\text{eff}}=300$; IQR and Beta-smoothed calibrators remain exploratory short-history remedies. Across 19 streams, an optional channel branch lifts injected single-sensor recall to $80.7\%\text{--}96.5\%$ while raising unmodified nominal causal $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$ from $1.5\%$ to $6.8\%\text{--}19.4\%$. Controlled ramp sweeps confirm excess causal detection vanishes once ramp duration reaches $L\ge 1024$ ($4C$), explaining GHL's $0.000$ Point-F1; pairing latent panels with raw-level trend monitors and testing natural sensor failures remain priorities for future work.
\end{enumerate}
