\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We studied whether Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures and multi-perspective circuit-breaker panels mitigate observation-space false-alarm flooding and 1D scalar score collapse across a 33-stream development cohort and 118 untouched held-out streams (within a 162-stream, 15-dataset archive):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Circuit-breaker panel, surgical quenching, and operational trade-offs:} Expanding $D=32$ whitened coordinates via a Reynolds-inspired multi-horizon decomposition into $224$ per-coordinate statistics with surgical weight-space quenching, nominal drift guards, prefix-routed attribution, and 32 onset relays prevents variance-dominating coordinates from inflating a shared scalar threshold. On the $n{=}19$ development streams, unquenched \emph{Reynolds 224-Bit} raises causal Point-F1 from $0.052$ to $0.138$ ($P=0.252$, $R=0.123$; $17/0$ wins, $p = 1.5{\times}10^{-5}$), while \emph{Laminar-JEPA} reaches $0.262$ buffered Point-F1 ($66.4\%$ ER) and cuts causal delay to $59.6$ steps ($62.5\%$ causal ER at $0.100$ causal F1). On the $118$ untouched held-out validation streams, the panel raises causal Point-F1 from $0.072\text{--}0.083$ to $0.143$ ($p = 1.6{\times}10^{-13}$, dataset-clustered $p = 0.007$) at $72.8\%$ recall, while surgical quenching cuts causal held-out FPR by $8.0\%$ relative across $162$ streams ($p = 0.0011$, dataset $p = 0.027$; median held-out FPR $1.03\%$).
    \item \emph{Mechanism isolation and multi-horizon scaling ($S \in \{1,\dots,64\}$):} Matched-backbone ablations isolate LayerNorm kurtosis compression ($\gamma_2: 6.10 \to 0.91$), learned latent targets ($D_{\text{KS}}: 0.376 \to 0.214$), barycenter centering (inter-block drift share $71.3\% \to 18.7\%$), and bias recentering versus precision tempering ($k_{\text{rogue}} \in [1, 4]$), while Proposition~\ref{prop:anti_collapse} characterizes the structural horizon-fluctuation concentration bound (with scalar $R_{\text{LT}}$ modulation yielding a null effect). Retraining the 224-bit panel and \emph{Laminar-JEPA} across $S \in \{1, 4, 16, 32, 64\}$ confirms $1.9\times\text{--}4.3\times$ higher causal Point-F1 and $+26\%\text{--}38\%$ higher event recall than 1D scalar scoring down to zero reporting lag ($S=1$) and 3-step lag ($S=4$).
    \item \emph{Applicability boundaries, calibration sample size, and single-channel branch:} The panel requires multichannel arrays ($K \ge 9$) and at least $N_{\text{eff}} \ge 300$ effective independent calibration observations ($N_{\text{cal}} \ge 1{,}000$ windows) for empirical quantiles ($\le 8\%$ bootstrap threshold CI half-width and $<3\%$ inter-segment FPR SD), or Robust-IQR / Beta-smoothed calibrators when $N_{\text{cal}} < 1{,}000$. Across 17 multivariate streams and six single-sensor fault classes, adding a complementary channel-level residual and variance branch raises single-channel event recall from $61.4\%\text{--}80.7\%$ to $93.0\%\text{--}98.2\%$ at the cost of higher nominal FPR, whereas slow process ramps (GHL, Metro) and online ramp adaptation remain future work.
\end{enumerate}
