\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Decomposing multi-horizon latent predictive errors into independently calibrated coordinate tripwires (\emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} at $128$ dials; \emph{Laminar-JEPA} at $224$ dials) yields three conclusions across $33$ design-stage and $118$ untouched archive streams:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Coordinate isolation and contextual coverage:} Per-dial tripwires prevent drifting coordinates from inflating a shared scalar threshold (Proposition~\ref{prop:hijacker_isolation}), catching in-range contextual and phase-desync faults that evade 1D SPOT and $\max_c|x_c|$ baselines \cite{wu2023flawed}. Across all $118$ untouched streams ($12/12$ LODO folds, $9/9$ datasets), \emph{Lite} lifts live causal ER from $52.5\%$ to $64.6\%$ ($p=0.0039$), halves delay ($69.0$ vs.\ $133.5$ steps) at $93\text{--}141$ windows/s on one CPU thread, and doubles Point-F1 when $N_{\text{cal}}\ge 500$ ($0.040\to 0.085$).
    \item \emph{Label-free operating point vs.\ ranking:} At post-hoc matched test FPR, the panel does not outrank scalar scoring. Its gain comes strictly from placing a label-free quorum at higher live episode coverage and shorter delay without test-label tuning.
    \item \emph{Concept-drift attribution and gating:} Block-permutation and Moving-Block Bootstrap audits show that $92.3\%$ of excess false alarms ($7.44\% \to 0.57\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$) come from post-calibration concept drift via a $3.1\times$ residual-covariance shift rather than tail estimation error, bounded by our five-step protocol and live $D_{\text{KS}}\ge 0.50$ recalibration ($24.3\%\text{--}32.0\%\to 4.8\%$ FPR).
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\emph{Future Work:} Three open directions follow directly from the failure boundaries measured here. First, low-rank or Riemannian streaming updates to $\mathbf{\Omega}$ and $\bar{\bm{\mu}}_{\text{ctx}}$ can track benign operating-mode switches without a $400$-window recalibration buffer. Second, conditioning our frozen-reference EWMA drift monitor on control inputs or multi-rate state priors is needed to retain its $58.5\%$ slow-ramp recall on GHL while suppressing false alarms from benign post-fault baseline wander ($74.4\%$ test FPR). Third, pairing the latent quorum with noise-adaptive coordinate scaling and low-FPR univariate checks can address uncalibrated sensor noise and $1/K$-diluted single-channel flatlines.
