\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Deploying \emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} centers on an integrated screening and fallback workflow rather than a distribution-free threshold guarantee. Streams passing $N_{\text{cal}}\ge 500$ and frozen $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}\le 5\%$ ($52/118$) run the $128$-dial panel, while the remaining $66$ short or drifting streams route to 1D SPOT fallback. Because conditioning on $\mathbf{X}_{\text{hold}}$ introduces selection optimism and finite-sample uncertainty ($5.8\%$ median dependence-adjusted $95\%$ UCB; $11.0\%$ mean and $3.9\%$ median independent $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$), accepted streams still require live $D_{\text{KS}}\ge 0.50$ monitoring with a $400$-window quarantine buffer awaiting confirmed mode transitions. On alarming windows, batched LOCO masking ranks channels by controlled perturbation sensitivity ($85.7\%$ Top-1 on injected shifts) as a triage aid rather than verified root-cause localization.

Per-stream healthy-prefix fitting suits telemetry spanning $K=2$ to $55$ channels with incompatible physical units, cadences, and regimes, where zero-shot pretrained weights struggle without fine-tuning. Prefix fitting transfers the frozen architecture, symmetric or horizon-preserving $\mathbf{I}_M\otimes\mathbf{\Omega}_D^{1/2}$ whitening, and calibration rule across domains at $93\text{--}141$ windows/s on one CPU thread without cross-asset label leakage. Null regularizer results, ranking parity with scalar JEPAs, and higher raw F1 for TimesNet and TranAD at $24.6\%\text{--}25.6\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ confirm that the gain comes from low-false-alarm operating-point placement, with \emph{Lite} trading $4.8$ recall points against $224$-dial \emph{Laminar-JEPA} for lower mean and buffered false alarms. Complementary guards remain necessary for $1/K$-diluted single-channel flatlines, unconditioned slow ramps, and prefixes below $500$ windows.

\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Integrating multi-horizon JEPA residuals, coordinate whitening, per-dial healthy tripwires, and fallback routing across $33$ primary development streams (from a $45$-stream pool) and $118$ untouched archive streams ($37+1+80=118$) yields three conclusions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Operating-point trade-off and whitening:} Under matched multiplicative scaling, per-dial tripwires prevent high-variance coordinates from inflating a shared threshold (Proposition~\ref{prop:hijacker_isolation}), and horizon-preserving Kronecker ZCA ($\mathbf{I}_M\otimes\mathbf{\Omega}_D^{1/2}$) matches dense ZCA across all $118$ streams ($0.101$ vs.\ $0.091$ F1, $p=0.76$). \emph{Lite} lifts causal ER over 1D JEPA from $52.5\%$ to $64.6\%$ ($p=0.0039$) and cuts latency from $133.5$ to $69.0$ steps, trading $-4.8$ ER points against $224$B for lower mean and buffered $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$, while doubling Point-F1 on $N_{\text{cal}}\ge 500$ ($p=0.047$).
    \item \emph{External baselines and synthetic attribution:} TimesNet and TranAD reach higher raw archive F1 only at $24.6\%\text{--}25.6\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ ($25.5\%\text{--}25.9\%$ vs.\ $51.2\%$ ER at $1\%$ test FPR), while matched-FPR ranking matches 1D JEPAs and LOCO masking provides synthetic perturbation attribution.
    \item \emph{Nonstationarity, screening, and quarantine:} Block permutation links $92.3\%$ of excess false alarms to chronological nonstationarity, strongly associated with covariance shift ($3.1\times$). Empirical screening ($52/118$ accepted, $66$ fallback) pairs with quarantined $D_{\text{KS}}\ge 0.50$ recalibration ($31.9\%$ vs.\ $9.1\%$ fault recall; $4.8\%$ confirmed FPR).
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\emph{Future Work:} Priorities include validating physical fault localization on field-labeled hardware failures, streaming updates to $\mathbf{\Omega}_D$ without $400$-window buffers, control-conditioned slow-ramp monitoring, and low-FPR univariate flatline guards.
