\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Operational deployment pairs healthy-prefix fitting ($K\in[2,55]$, no cross-asset weights) with archive-informed gating validated via nested LODO screening. Two-stage screening retains $52$ streams ($11.0\%$ mean $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$) and drift screening ($D_{\text{KS}}\le 0.50$) retains $46$ ($6.8\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$), routing remaining streams to 1D SPOT while holding live $D_{\text{KS}}\ge 0.50$ shifts in a $400$-window quarantine buffer. Post-alarm LOCO masking provides synthetic perturbation triage rather than field localization.

Overall, null regularizer findings, matched-FPR ranking parity, and high raw FPR for TimesNet and TranAD show that the panel's benefit rests on automated label-free operating-point selection, rescuing scalar under-triggering on stable long histories ($49.8\%$ vs.\ $32.1\%$ ER across $46$ accepted streams) rather than superior intrinsic ranking. Deployment spans three tiers: (1)~\emph{standalone fit} on abrupt/contextual faults passing $N_{\text{cal}}\ge 500$, $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}\le 5\%$, and $D_{\text{KS}}\le 0.50$; (2)~\emph{complementary guards required} on diluted single-sensor flatlines, slow ramps, and uncalibrated switches; and (3)~\emph{1D SPOT fallback} when prefixes are short or drift screens fail.

\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Combining multi-horizon JEPA residuals, whitening, per-dial tripwires, and fallback routing across $33$ dev and $118$ archive streams supports three conclusions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Label-free operating-point selection:} Rather than better underlying discrimination, the quorum shifts the label-free operating point. Idealized equivariance holds approximately under additive floors, Kronecker ZCA matches dense ZCA within CIs, and consistent PCA recovers causal shrinkage drops. Unscreened \emph{Lite} lifts causal ER ($52.5\%\to 64.6\%$, $p_{\text{clus}}=0.0039$) and cuts segment-paired delay ($-41.4$ steps; stream-paired $-74.6$), while raising test FPR ($8.8\%\to 17.0\%$) and leaving dataset Point-F1 mixed ($0.076\to 0.086$).
    \item \emph{Matched budgets and baselines:} Matched-FPR checks confirm ranking parity with scalar JEPAs (1D leads F1 at $f=1\%$), whereas TimesNet and TranAD gain F1 only at $24.6\%\text{--}25.6\%$ FPR. LOCO masking hits $85.7\%$ Top-1 synthetic subset sensitivity.
    \item \emph{Complete prospective trade-off:} Healthy-block permutation reduces archive FPR by $92.3\%$. Evaluated completely across $118$ streams, three-stage routing trades $10.0\%$ vs.\ $8.8\%$ test FPR ($12.9$ vs.\ $8.8$ episodes per thousand healthy steps) against complete 1D SPOT for $59.4\%$ vs.\ $52.5\%$ ER and $0.091$ vs.\ $0.076$ F1, paired with quarantined drift monitoring.
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\emph{Future Work:} Next steps include field root-cause validation, lightweight online $\mathbf{\Omega}_D$ updates, control-conditioned ramp guards, and flatline monitoring.
