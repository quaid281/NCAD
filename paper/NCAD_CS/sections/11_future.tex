\section{Conclusion and Future Horizons}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced Flow-JEPA, a continuous self-supervised framework that reformulates unsupervised multivariate anomaly detection as vector field modeling on a regularized latent dynamical manifold. By moving beyond raw-space ODE integration and eliminating signal-level reconstruction traps, Flow-JEPA models physical systems as smooth, continuous tangent velocity fields. Non-contrastive manifold regularization enforces representation diversity while decorrelating latent dynamical modes. Coupled with a Deterministic Midpoint Velocity discrepancy metric, Mahalanobis covariance whitening, and Extreme Value Theory (EVT / SPOT) tail calibration, Flow-JEPA establishes asymptotically sound, threshold-free anomaly alarms.

Empirical evaluations across 11 diverse benchmark datasets comprising 130 telemetry channels (and extended distributed streaming ablations on Exathlon, totaling over 1,300 model runs) establish that Flow-JEPA consistently outperforms baseline contrastive and reconstructive architectures (TimesNet, TranAD, Anomaly Transformer, DCdetector, and NCAD) across both channel-weighted and unweighted dataset macro metrics. Systematic ablations confirm that Optimal Transport Flow Matching resolves conditional-mean collapse on multi-branching dynamics and provides robust stability against non-stationary streaming drift.

From an operational perspective, while strict timestep-level Point-F1 scores in the $0.20-0.35$ range reflect the low base-rate and severe stochastic noise of continuous industrial sensor streams, Flow-JEPA's $+52.5\%$ relative improvement over reconstructive baselines transforms anomaly detection from high-fatigue alarm floods into a high-confidence prioritization and triage mechanism for human operators.

Promising avenues for future research include:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep, topsep=0pt, leftmargin=*]
    \item \textit{Differentiable Physical Constraint Layers:} For specialized physical domains where governing laws are known \textit{a priori} (e.g., Kirchhoff circuit laws in power grids or kinematics in robotics), integrating differentiable physical constraint layers directly into the Flow-JEPA latent velocity field to guarantee exact boundary compliance.
    \item \textit{Continuous Online Streaming Adaptation:} Developing continuous test-time momentum updates to track gradual seasonal or orbital regime drifts without catastrophic forgetting.
    \item \textit{Edge Hardware Quantization:} Deploying lightweight integer-quantized (INT8) Flow-JEPA encoders directly on radiation-hardened spacecraft microcontrollers and wearable biomedical sensors.
    \item \textit{Foundation Models for Cyber-Physical Telemetry:} Pretraining large-scale multi-domain Flow-JEPA world models on massive multi-mission telemetry archives for zero-shot downstream fault diagnosis.
\end{itemize}
