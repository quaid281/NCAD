\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We evaluated how Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures condition anomaly residuals under automated extreme-value calibration across 33 continuous telemetry streams under a chronological held-out protocol:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Factorial Mechanism Isolation and Multi-Horizon Whitening:} Matched-encoder ablations decouple score compression from representation learning: LayerNorm bottlenecks bound residual kurtosis ($\gamma_2: 6.10 \to 0.91$), learned latent projections halve calibration-to-holdout drift ($D_{\text{KS}}: 0.376 \to 0.214$ in TS-JEPA), and multi-horizon barycenter whitening in Soliton-Reynolds JEPA lowers drift further to $D_{\text{KS}} = 0.179$ with $0.6\%$ causal FPR. On the fixed backbone, post-hoc Lieb--Thirring soliton modulation satisfies TOST equivalence ($\Delta = \pm 0.020$, $p_{\text{TOST}} = 2.7{\times}10^{-5}$), and short horizons ($S \in \{1, 4\}$) eliminate the 63-step emission lag.
    \item \emph{Relative False-Alarm Control and Hybrid Quiet-Fault Recovery:} Latent scoring lowers held-out false alarms three- to four-fold under buffered attribution and ten- to forty-fold causally. Soliton-Reynolds JEPA matches TS-JEPA on buffered PR-AUC ($0.331$) and leads in causal matched-FPR event recall ($f^* = 0.01$: $0.562$ vs.\ $0.217$ for TimesNet), trading lower causal SPOT Point-F1 ($0.050$ at a $2.4\%$ test alert rate vs.\ $0.161$ at $10.8\%$ for TS-JEPA) for $0.6\%$ held-out FPR, while a hybrid variance monitor recovers quiet single-channel faults ($0.42\text{--}0.50$ event recall).
    \item \emph{Calibration Drift and Adaptive Thresholding:} Static SPOT overshoots $q = 10^{-3}$ by $46\text{--}55\times$ under $S=64$ buffered smearing and $15\times$ causally for TS-JEPA, whereas barycenter-whitened Soliton-Reynolds JEPA reduces causal overshoot to $4\times$ ($0.38\%$ stream-macro FPR across 33 streams) and cuts the inter-block drift share four-fold ($71.3\% \to 18.7\%$). Runs-declustered SPOT and rolling quantiles narrow the remaining gap across step and variance shifts, motivating regime-conditioned conformal calibration.
\end{enumerate}
