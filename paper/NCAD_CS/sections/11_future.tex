\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}
Operational deployment pairs healthy-prefix fitting across $K\in[2,55]$ channels with fold-validated fallback gates (Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}(a)). Two-stage screening keeps $52$ streams ($11.0\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$), and drift screening keeps $46$ ($6.8\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$). Remaining streams, including all $40$ SMAP and $12$ of $13$ MSL below $N_{\text{cal}}=500$, route to 1D SPOT while alarms remain active during $400$-window quarantine.

The panel excels at label-free operating-point selection under fold-validated gates ($49.8\%$ vs.\ $32.1\%$ ER on $46$ streams) rather than better intrinsic ranking. Under constrained cost weighting (Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}(c)), the hybrid targets recall-prioritized settings ($w\le 5.92$), whereas false-alarm aversion ($w\ge 6\text{--}20$) favors 1D SPOT. Deployment spans three tiers: (1)~\emph{standalone fit} passing all gates; (2)~\emph{complementary guards} on diluted flatlines, ramps, and mode switches; and (3)~\emph{1D SPOT fallback} on short or drifting streams.

\section{Conclusion and Future Work}
\label{sec:conclusion}
Evaluations across $33$ dev and $118$ archive streams support three conclusions:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Label-free operating point:} Rather than improving score ranking, the quorum shifts the operating point under multiplicative-matching floors ($99.96\%$ agreement); Kronecker ZCA matches dense ZCA within CIs and consistent PCA recovers causal drops. Unscreened \emph{Lite} raises archive ER ($52.5\%\to 64.6\%$, $p_{\text{clus}}=0.0039$) and cuts segment-paired delay ($-41.4$ steps; $-74.6$ stream-paired), raising test FPR ($8.8\%\to 17.0\%$) with mixed Point-F1 ($0.076\to 0.086$).
    \item \emph{Matched budgets and baselines:} Scalar JEPAs match ranking (1D leads F1 at $f=1\%$), whereas TimesNet and TranAD gain F1 only at $24.6\%\text{--}25.6\%$ FPR. LOCO masking hits $85.7\%$ Top-1 synthetic sensitivity.
    \item \emph{Recall-prioritized prospective trade-off:} Shuffling healthy blocks breaks chronological order and cuts archive FPR by $92.3\%$. With fold-validated gates and mode-confirmed quarantine across $118$ streams, three-stage routing trades $10.0\%$ vs.\ $8.8\%$ test FPR ($12.9$ vs.\ $8.8$ episodes/1k steps) against 1D SPOT for $59.4\%$ ER and $0.091$ F1 (vs.\ $52.5\%$ and $0.076$; $p_{\text{clus}}=0.0156$).
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\emph{Future Work:} Four gaps guide next steps. Perturbation attribution needs field root-cause validation beyond synthetic injections, paired with low-FPR univariate flatline monitors for $1/K$-diluted single-sensor faults. Lightweight online $\mathbf{\Omega}_D$ updates after confirmed mode switches can replace $400$-window buffers, while control-conditioned drift monitors can curb slow-ramp wander.
