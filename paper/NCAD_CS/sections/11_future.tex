\section{Conclusion and Future Horizons}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced \textbf{TS-JEPA} (Time-Series Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture), a self-supervised framework that reformulates unsupervised multivariate anomaly detection as non-contrastive latent world model forecasting. By eliminating raw signal-level reconstruction loss and artificial negative sampling, TS-JEPA models physical systems as smooth, continuous limit-cycle attractors in representation space. Non-contrastive VICReg regularization enforces representation diversity while decorrelating latent dynamical modes. Coupled with a Covariance-Whitened (Mahalanobis) discrepancy metric and Extreme Value Theory (EVT / SPOT) tail calibration, TS-JEPA normalizes directional variance and establishes mathematically grounded anomaly thresholds.

Empirical evaluations across 26 benchmark channels spanning five distinct physical domains (NASA spacecraft telemetry, distributed cloud streaming logs, environmental IoT, biomedical gait dynamics, and high-dimensional wearables) establish that TS-JEPA consistently outperforms baseline contrastive and reconstructive architectures, achieving a \textbf{+35.6\% grand macro PA-F1 gain} (0.3786 vs. 0.2793). Systematic ablation sweeps confirm that VICReg prevents catastrophic representation collapse while target momentum EMA provides vital stability on non-stationary streams.

Promising avenues for future research include:
\begin{itemize}[noitemsep, topsep=0pt, leftmargin=*]
    \item \textbf{Continuous Online Streaming Adaptation:} Developing continuous test-time momentum updates to track gradual seasonal or orbital regime drifts without catastrophic forgetting.
    \item \textbf{Edge Hardware Quantization:} Deploying lightweight integer-quantized (INT8) TCN-JEPA encoders directly on radiation-hardened spacecraft microcontrollers and wearable biomedical sensors.
    \item \textbf{Foundation Models for Cyber-Physical Telemetry:} Pretraining large-scale multi-domain TS-JEPA world models on massive multi-mission telemetry archives for zero-shot downstream fault diagnosis.
\end{itemize}
