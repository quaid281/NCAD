\section{Multi-Sensor IoT and Remote Sensing Benchmark Suite}
\label{sec:dataset}

Our benchmark suite comprises 11 datasets spanning 45 continuous telemetry streams sourced from NASA spacecraft archives \cite{Hundman2018b}, the UCI Machine Learning Repository \cite{bachlin2010wearable, asuncion2007uci}, TimeEval \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}, and mTSBench \cite{zhou2026mtsbench}: 33 streams are evaluated under the primary multi-seed held-out calibration protocol and 12 reserved for operational case studies. Streams span four physical domains: \emph{spacecraft telemetry} (SMAP Earth-observation \texttt{A-1}/\texttt{A-2}/\texttt{A-7}/\texttt{D-3}/\texttt{P-3}/\texttt{E-1} power, thermal, and attitude subsystems; MSL rover \texttt{C-1}/\texttt{C-2}/\texttt{D-14}/\texttt{D-15}/\texttt{D-16}/\texttt{M-1} actuator and environmental channels, with two short-nominal streams reserved; SWAN-SF solar magnetograms \cite{angryk2020multivariate}); \emph{wearable kinematics} (six Daphnet FoG streams \cite{bachlin2010wearable}: 64~Hz tri-axial accelerometry for Freezing-of-Gait events, so $S{=}64$ is a 1-s horizon); \emph{industrial processes} (14 GHL gasoil heating-loop streams, the Genesis pick-and-place testbed, and GECCO water-distribution chemistry/hydrology at 1-minute resolution); and \emph{smart infrastructure} (Room-Occupancy environmental sensing \cite{candanedo2016accurate}, six CICIDS edge network-flow streams, CalIt2 people-flow \cite{asuncion2007uci}, and Metro I-94 highway traffic).

Each stream provides nominal training sequences and labeled anomaly test sequences; channels are standardized by training statistics with $\bm{\sigma}$ floored at $10^{-2}$ against flatline phases. We evaluate the standardized subsets studied in the literature \cite{Hundman2018b, schmidl2022anomaly} (of 55 SMAP and 27 MSL archive entities) for strict matching and reproducible runtime.

To eliminate circular in-sample calibration, each stream's nominal sequence is partitioned chronologically into training (65\%), calibration (17.5\%), and held-out evaluation (17.5\%) blocks separated by an isolation gap (Section~\ref{subsec:exp_setup}). The held-out protocol spans 33 streams across seven datasets (6 Daphnet, 14 GHL, 4 MSL, 6 SMAP, Genesis, Metro, SWAN); the remaining 12 streams are reserved as operational case studies (CalIt2, two Room-Occupancy, two short-nominal MSL, GECCO, and six CICIDS) because their nominal blocks are too short for disjoint partitions ($N_{\text{cal}} < 500$) or, for the 80-channel CICIDS network flows, high-dimensional stride-1 evaluation across multi-seed runs was computationally prohibitive compared to lower-dimensional physical telemetry. The dyadic front-end ablation (Table~\ref{tab:ablations}b) uses a separate five-stream panel that additionally draws one SMD channel outside this manifest.

Two caveats bound interpretation. SMAP and MSL contain documented mislabeled segments and near-trivial anomalies \cite{wu2023flawed}; their per-dataset numbers are upper bounds on detectability. Entity coverage is also incomplete (6 of 55 SMAP and 4 of 27 MSL channels, selected by nominal-block length and stride-1 runtime constraints, not labels), so claims apply to the evaluated entities, not the full archives.

Supplementary Table~\ref{tab:dataset_specs} and the benchmark stream manifest enumerate the suite: $2{,}079{,}507$ nominal training and $2{,}986{,}574$ test timesteps ($>$5.06M observations across 33 primary and 12 case-study streams), recording channel dimensions, contamination ratios, anomaly typologies, split lengths, and evaluated seeds. All counts in this paper reconcile against this manifest.
