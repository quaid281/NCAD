\section{Multi-Domain Benchmark Datasets}
\label{sec:dataset}

To evaluate the universal applicability of Flow-JEPA across diverse physical, cyber-physical, biomedical, and distributed dynamics, our primary multi-domain benchmark suite spans 11 real-world datasets comprising 130 continuous telemetry channels sourced from NASA telemetry archives \cite{Hundman2018b}, the PhysioNet biomedical database \cite{bachlin2010wearable}, and the mTSBench multi-domain repository \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*]
    \item \textit{NASA SMAP Spacecraft Telemetry \cite{Hundman2018b} (51 channels):} Continuous telemetry channels from the Soil Moisture Active Passive (SMAP) satellite tracking solar array currents, bus voltages, battery temperatures, and thruster status subject to radiation upsets and operational state switches.
    \item \textit{NASA MSL Curiosity Rover Telemetry \cite{Hundman2018b} (26 channels):} Environmental and robotic actuator telemetry from the Mars Science Laboratory (MSL) rover monitoring motor temperatures, joint angles, and power subsystem faults.
    \item \textit{Daphnet Parkinsonian Gait Dynamics \cite{bachlin2010wearable} (26 channels):} High-frequency tri-axial accelerometry from wearable body sensor nodes (shank, thigh, trunk; 9 continuous dimensions per subject) tracking Freezing-of-Gait (FoG) breakdown events in Parkinson's patients.
    \item \textit{GHL Separation Plant (14 channels):} Multi-phase gas-oil-water separation industrial process telemetry monitoring tank levels, flow rates, and valve control faults.
    \item \textit{CICIDS Network Intrusion (6 channels):} High-throughput network flow telemetry capturing protocol dynamics and security anomalies (DDoS bursts and port scans).
    \item \textit{Room-Occupancy IoT \cite{candanedo2016accurate} (2 channels):} Environmental IoT sensing ($\text{CO}_2$, light, temperature, humidity) tracking human activity transitions.
    \item \textit{GECCO Water Quality (1 channel):} Physical water distribution process telemetry monitoring chemical and hydrological measurements.
    \item \textit{Genesis Demonstrator (1 channel):} Industrial manufacturing testbed telemetry tracking automated handling machinery faults.
    \item \textit{SWAN Hydrology (1 channel):} Surface water and marine wave telemetry tracking sudden coastal surges.
    \item \textit{CalIt2 Traffic Monitoring (1 channel):} Vehicle flow counts and speed telemetry at campus entry points during special events.
    \item \textit{Metro Traffic Flow (1 channel):} Interstate highway traffic volume and environmental conditions.
\end{enumerate}

In addition to the 130 primary benchmark channels, we evaluate the \textit{Exathlon Distributed Cloud Benchmark \cite{jacob2021exathlon} (30 channels)} in our ablation suite (Section~\ref{sec:ablations}). Exathlon captures high-dimensional execution traces from Apache Spark clusters (JVM heap utilization, garbage collection pauses, memory leaks, and CPU thrashing), providing a rigorous testbed for target encoder momentum stability under non-stationary streaming drift.

Each benchmark channel provides pre-split training sequences (consisting exclusively of nominal operational behavior) and testing sequences containing labeled ground-truth anomalies. Continuous numeric features are standardized via training statistics ($\bm{\mu}_{\text{train}}, \bm{\sigma}_{\text{train}}$) using robust epsilon floors $\max(\bm{\sigma}, 10^{-2})$ to prevent numerical instability during flat-line operational phases.
