\section{Multi-Sensor Telemetry Benchmark Suite}
\label{sec:dataset}

Each test case is a \emph{stream}: one multi-sensor recording ($K$ channels) comprising an unlabeled healthy history followed by a labeled test segment. Two cohorts play distinct roles. From a 45-stream development pool across 11 datasets, 33 qualifying streams formed the primary development cohort, while 141 archive streams across 12 datasets (118 never inspected during design) provide the primary evaluation.

\noindent\emph{Development pool and qualifying cohort:} Drawn from NASA spacecraft archives \cite{Hundman2018b}, the University of California, Irvine (UCI) Machine Learning Repository \cite{bachlin2010wearable, asuncion2007uci}, TimeEval \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}, and mTSBench \cite{zhou2026mtsbench}, the 45-stream pool spans $K\in[2,72]$ channels across three domains:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Spacecraft and solar:} six SMAP ($K{=}25$) and six MSL ($K{=}55$) streams divided by NASA into out-of-range \emph{point} and in-range \emph{contextual} faults \cite{Hundman2018b}, plus SWAN-SF solar magnetograms \cite{angryk2020multivariate} ($K{=}38$).
    \item \emph{Wearables:} six $64\,\text{Hz}$ Daphnet Freezing-of-Gait (FoG) streams \cite{bachlin2010wearable} ($K{=}9$, $72$ episodes, where $S{=}64$ spans $1\,\text{s}$).
    \item \emph{Industrial and infrastructure:} 14 slow-ramp Gasoil Heating Loop (GHL) streams ($K{=}16$, $26$ faults), Genesis ($K{=}18$), GECCO ($K{=}9$), Metro I-94 ($K{=}5$), Room-Occupancy \cite{candanedo2016accurate} ($K{=}5$), CalIt2 \cite{asuncion2007uci} ($K{=}2$), and six CICIDS streams ($K{=}72$).
\end{enumerate}
Thirty-three streams ($19$ non-GHL and $14$ GHL across $7$ datasets) satisfy $N_{\text{cal}} \ge 500$, $K\le 55$, and $\le 1.2{\times}10^5$ test steps to form the primary development cohort in Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(c). Five shorter streams ($764\text{--}1{,}492$ steps), six high-channel CICIDS streams, and GECCO are held aside or evaluated in the archive.

\noindent\emph{Evaluation archive:} The 141-stream archive collects every stream across 12 datasets with at least $100$ calibration windows and one labeled fault ($1{,}920\text{--}132{,}481$ healthy steps and $2{,}158\text{--}114{,}001$ test steps). Multi-stream sets cover SMAP ($47$), Daphnet ($22$), MSL ($18$), SMD ($18$), Exathlon ($17$), and OPPORTUNITY ($13$), alongside single-stream GECCO, Genesis, Pooled Server Metrics (PSM), CreditCard, Metro, and SWAN. Excluding the $23$ tuning streams leaves $118$ untouched streams across $9$ datasets ($37 + 1 + 80 = 118$): $37$ streams from $4$ strictly zero-tuning external sets (SMD, Exathlon, PSM, CreditCard), $1$ archive-heldout GECCO stream excluded from tuning, and $80$ unseen streams from $4$ partially tuned sets (SMAP, MSL, Daphnet, OPPORTUNITY; Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}(b)).

\noindent\emph{Per-stream fitting and transfer:} Each stream fits independently on its own nominal prefix, requiring a healthy warm-up history rather than pretrained-weight transfer. What transfers zero-tune across heterogeneous sensor counts ($K\in[2,55]$) is the frozen architecture, damping, tripwire percentile, and quorum rule across disjoint calibration blocks (Section~\ref{subsec:exp_setup}).

\noindent\emph{Caveats:} Because SMAP and MSL have documented labeling flaws \cite{wu2023flawed} and same-facility streams share hardware traits, we combine dataset-clustered inference ($7$ dev, $9$ untouched sets), separate zero-tuning vs.\ tuned cohorts, nested leave-one-dataset-out (LODO) tuning, and 9-fold exclusion sensitivity (Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}).
