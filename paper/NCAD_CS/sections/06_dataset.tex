\section{Multi-Sensor Internet-of-Things and Remote Sensing Benchmark Suite}
\label{sec:dataset}

Our benchmark suite comprises 11 datasets spanning 45 continuous telemetry streams from NASA spacecraft archives \cite{Hundman2018b}, the University of California, Irvine (UCI) Machine Learning Repository \cite{bachlin2010wearable, asuncion2007uci}, TimeEval \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}, and mTSBench \cite{zhou2026mtsbench}, expanded in archive-wide evaluations to 141 qualifying multivariate streams across 12 datasets and 162 streams across 15 datasets (Table~\ref{tab:cohorts}). Thirty-three streams enter the primary protocol (19 non-GHL development streams and 14 GHL streams); 12 are reserved with explicit accounting below. The suite spans four domains: \emph{Spacecraft and solar telemetry} covers six SMAP streams ($K{=}25$ channels), six MSL rover streams ($K{=}55$, four primary and two short-nominal), and SWAN-SF solar magnetograms \cite{angryk2020multivariate} ($K{=}38$), where NASA's archive \cite{Hundman2018b} classifies each SMAP/MSL sequence as an out-of-range \emph{point} fault or in-range \emph{contextual} fault (Table~\ref{tab:point_vs_contextual}(a)). \emph{Wearable kinematics} covers six Daphnet Freezing-of-Gait (FoG) streams \cite{bachlin2010wearable} at $64\,\text{Hz}$ ($K{=}9$, $72$ episodes, where $S{=}64$ equals $1\,\text{s}$). \emph{Industrial and infrastructure telemetry} covers 14 Gasoil Heating Loop (GHL) streams ($K{=}16$, $26$ faults), the Genesis pick-and-place testbed ($K{=}18$), GECCO water chemistry at $1$-minute resolution ($K{=}9$, $37$ anomalies), Metro I-94 hourly traffic ($K{=}5$), Room-Occupancy sensing \cite{candanedo2016accurate} ($K{=}5$), CalIt2 building flow \cite{asuncion2007uci} ($K{=}2$), and six CICIDS network streams ($K{=}72$). Section~\ref{subsec:gating_ablation} evaluates six single-channel fault classes across all 17 multivariate development streams ($K > 1$).

Each stream provides nominal training sequences ($N_{\text{tr}} \in [2{,}074, 120{,}000]$) and labeled test sequences ($N_{\text{test}} \in [2{,}158, 114{,}001]$). Channels are standardized by training statistics with $\bm{\sigma}$ floored at $10^{-2}$. Each nominal sequence is split chronologically into training ($65\%$), nominal calibration ($17.5\%$), and held-out nominal evaluation ($17.5\%$) blocks separated by a 64-step isolation gap (Section~\ref{subsec:exp_setup}). Primary 33-stream inclusion required $N_{\text{cal}} \ge 500$ ($T_{\text{nom}} \ge 3{,}769$) and $K \le 55$, $N_{\text{test}} \le 1.2\times 10^5$ (Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(c)); among the 12 reserved streams, five have $T_{\text{nom}} \in [764, 1{,}492]$ ($N_{\text{cal}} \le 101$ across CalIt2, MSL C-2, MSL D-16, and two Room-Occupancy streams), six CICIDS streams have $K{=}72, N_{\text{test}} \ge 1.36\times 10^5$, and GECCO ($K{=}9$) enters the archive cohorts. To eliminate subset-selection bias, Table~\ref{tab:calibrator_comparison} evaluates all 141 qualifying multivariate streams across 12 datasets ($N_{\text{cal}} \ge 100$ and $\ge 1$ test anomaly: 47 SMAP, 22 Daphnet, 18 MSL, 18 SMD, 17 Exathlon, 13 OPPORTUNITY, GECCO, Genesis, Pooled Server Metrics (PSM), CreditCard, Metro, and SWAN) at three seeds, of which 118 streams were never inspected during development, and evaluates four-horizon Laminar-JEPA across 162 streams spanning 15 datasets (adding 14 GHL, Secure Water Treatment (SWaT), Water Distribution (WADI), and reserve streams; 139 untouched streams).

Two caveats bound external validity: (i)~SMAP and MSL contain documented labeling flaws \cite{wu2023flawed}; and (ii)~streams within multi-stream archives share hardware platforms. We therefore treat dataset clusters ($N \in \{7, 9, 12, 15\}$) as co-primary statistical units alongside streams, reporting Dataset-Macro means, cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals (CI), exact dataset sign-flip permutation tests, and leave-one-dataset-out (LODO) verification (Section~\ref{subsec:exp_setup}).
