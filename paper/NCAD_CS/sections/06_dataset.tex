\section{Multi-Sensor IoT and Remote Sensing Benchmark Suite}
\label{sec:dataset}

To evaluate our Physics-Informed JEPA framework across diverse edge, cyber-physical, and orbital sensing dynamics, our benchmark suite comprises 11 datasets spanning 45 strictly matched continuous telemetry channels sourced from NASA spacecraft archives \cite{Hundman2018b}, the PhysioNet biomedical database \cite{bachlin2010wearable}, TimeEval \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}, and mTSBench \cite{zhou2026mtsbench}. We organize these channels into four physical sensing domains:

\subsection{Domain 1: Satellite and Spacecraft Telemetry (13 Channels)}
This domain encompasses high-reliability orbital and deep-space telemetry. The \textit{NASA SMAP Earth Observation Satellite} \cite{Hundman2018b} (6 evaluated channels) provides continuous electrical power, thermal, and attitude telemetry from the Soil Moisture Active Passive spacecraft, monitoring solar array currents, bus voltages, battery temperatures, and thruster statuses subject to radiation upsets and operational mode shifts. The \textit{NASA MSL Curiosity Rover} \cite{Hundman2018b} (6 evaluated channels) tracks environmental and robotic actuator streams from the Mars Science Laboratory rover, recording drive motor currents, joint angles, arm kinematics, and power subsystem faults during planetary surface operations. Finally, the \textit{SWAN-SF Space Weather Telemetry} \cite{angryk2020multivariate} (1 channel) provides solar photospheric vector magnetogram telemetry from NASA SDO/HMI, tracking active region magnetic field perturbations during solar eruptive events.

\subsection{Domain 2: Wearable Biometrics and Kinematics (6 Channels)}
This domain evaluates human kinematic telemetry via the \textit{Daphnet Wearable Body Sensor Network} \cite{bachlin2010wearable} (6 channels). Telemetry consists of high-frequency tri-axial accelerometry recorded from wearable sensor nodes placed on the shank, thigh, and trunk to track Freezing-of-Gait (FoG) motor breakdown events in Parkinson's disease patients.

\subsection{Domain 3: Industrial Processes and Cyber-Physical Systems (16 Channels)}
This domain covers continuous industrial processes and fluid transportation. The \textit{GHL Gasoil Heating Loop} (14 channels) captures thermal and fluid process loop telemetry, monitoring temperatures, flow rates, and valve control dynamics. The \textit{Genesis Automation Testbed} (1 channel) tracks manufacturing automation telemetry from an industrial pick-and-place demonstrator during mechanical handling faults. The \textit{GECCO Water Distribution IoT} (1 channel) monitors physical water distribution infrastructure through continuous chemical and hydrological sensor measurements.

\subsection{Domain 4: Smart Infrastructure and Environmental Sensing (10 Channels)}
This domain evaluates urban infrastructure and cyber-physical networks. The \textit{Room-Occupancy Environmental IoT} dataset \cite{candanedo2016accurate} (2 channels) records ambient environmental conditions ($\text{CO}_2$, light, temperature, humidity) tracking room-occupancy state switches. The \textit{CICIDS Edge Network Telemetry} (6 channels) captures high-throughput network flow dynamics, tracking gateway protocol shifts and security anomalies including DDoS bursts and port scans. The \textit{CalIt2 Building People-Flow Telemetry} \cite{asuncion2007uci} (1 channel) records continuous pedestrian counts via optical beam sensors at the UC Irvine Calit2 facility entrance. Finally, the \textit{Metro Highway Traffic} dataset (1 channel) tracks interstate highway traffic volume and environmental flow conditions on Minnesota Interstate I-94.

Each benchmark channel provides pre-split training sequences (consisting exclusively of nominal operational behavior) and testing sequences containing labeled ground-truth anomalies. Continuous numeric features are standardized via training statistics ($\bm{\mu}_{\text{train}}, \bm{\sigma}_{\text{train}}$), flooring standard deviations at $\max(\bm{\sigma}, 10^{-2})$ to prevent division by zero during flat-line operational phases. While raw mission repositories contain larger telemetry sets (e.g., SMAP encompasses 55 telemetry channels and MSL encompasses 27 channels across various subsystems), our benchmark evaluates the standardized 6-channel subsets commonly evaluated in the multi-sensor benchmark literature \cite{Hundman2018b, schmidl2022anomaly} to ensure strict matching and reproducible runtime conditions across all baselines.
