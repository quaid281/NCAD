\section{Multi-Sensor Telemetry Benchmark Suite}
\label{sec:dataset}

Each test case is a \emph{stream}: one multi-sensor recording ($K$ channels) comprising an unlabeled healthy history followed by a labeled test segment. Two cohorts play distinct roles. Forty-five streams across 11 datasets guided design, while 141 archive streams across 12 datasets (118 never inspected during design) provide the primary evaluation.

\noindent\emph{Development suite:} Drawn from NASA spacecraft archives \cite{Hundman2018b}, the University of California, Irvine (UCI) Machine Learning Repository \cite{bachlin2010wearable, asuncion2007uci}, TimeEval \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}, and mTSBench \cite{zhou2026mtsbench} ($K\in[2,72]$), the 45 streams span three domains:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Spacecraft and solar:} six SMAP ($K{=}25$) and six MSL ($K{=}55$) streams, split by NASA into out-of-range \emph{point} and in-range \emph{contextual} faults \cite{Hundman2018b}, plus SWAN-SF solar magnetograms \cite{angryk2020multivariate} ($K{=}38$).
    \item \emph{Wearables:} six $64\,\text{Hz}$ Daphnet Freezing-of-Gait (FoG) streams \cite{bachlin2010wearable} ($K{=}9$, $72$ episodes; $S{=}64$ spans $1\,\text{s}$).
    \item \emph{Industrial and infrastructure:} 14 slow-ramp Gasoil Heating Loop (GHL) streams ($K{=}16$, $26$ faults), Genesis ($K{=}18$), GECCO ($K{=}9$), Metro I-94 ($K{=}5$), Room-Occupancy \cite{candanedo2016accurate} ($K{=}5$), CalIt2 \cite{asuncion2007uci} ($K{=}2$), and six CICIDS streams ($K{=}72$).
\end{enumerate}
Thirty-three streams ($19$ non-GHL and $14$ GHL across $7$ datasets) meet $N_{\text{cal}} \ge 500$, $K\le 55$, and $\le 1.2{\times}10^5$ test steps in Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(c). The remaining $12$ ($5$ short histories of $764\text{--}1{,}492$ steps, $6$ high-channel CICIDS streams, and GECCO) are held aside or evaluated in the archive.

\noindent\emph{Evaluation archive:} The archive contains every qualifying stream from 12 datasets with at least $100$ calibration windows and one labeled anomaly. Multi-stream cohorts comprise SMAP ($47$), Daphnet ($22$), MSL ($18$), SMD ($18$), Exathlon ($17$), and OPPORTUNITY ($13$), alongside single-stream GECCO, Genesis, Pooled Server Metrics (PSM), CreditCard, Metro, and SWAN. Healthy histories span $1{,}920\text{--}132{,}481$ steps and test segments span $2{,}158\text{--}114{,}001$ steps; removing the $23$ tuning streams leaves $118$ untouched streams across $9$ datasets (Section~\ref{subsec:cohort_repro}).

\noindent\emph{Per-stream fitting and zero-tune transfer:} Every stream is fitted independently on its own unlabeled nominal prefix, so deploying on a new physical asset requires a healthy warm-up history rather than cross-asset weight transfer. What transfers zero-tune across heterogeneous sensor counts ($K\in[2,55]$) and sampling rates is the architecture, whitening damping, tripwire percentile, and quorum rule. In fixed-epoch development runs, the first $65\%$ of nominal data fits weights and standardization ($\bm{\sigma}$ floored at $10^{-2}$), the next $17.5\%$ calibrates thresholds ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$), and after a $64$-step gap the final $17.5\%$ checks held-out false alarms ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{hold}}$). Primary archive runs subdivide the fitting prefix into disjoint selection and adaptation blocks (Section~\ref{subsec:exp_setup}).

\noindent\emph{Caveats:} SMAP and MSL contain documented labeling flaws \cite{wu2023flawed}, and streams from one facility share hardware characteristics, distinguishing cross-stream generalization within an archive from cross-domain generalization to unseen sensor suites. We therefore evaluate both dataset-clustered inference ($7$ dev, $9$ untouched, and $12$ full-archive datasets) and 12-fold leave-one-dataset-out (LODO) audits (Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}).
