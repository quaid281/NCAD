\section{Multi-Sensor Telemetry Benchmark Suite}
\label{sec:dataset}

Each test case is a \emph{stream}: one multi-sensor recording ($K$ channels) comprising an unlabeled healthy history followed by a labeled test segment. Two cohorts play distinct roles. From a 45-stream development pool across 11 datasets, 33 qualifying streams formed the primary development cohort, while 141 archive streams across 12 datasets (118 never inspected during design) provide the primary evaluation.

\noindent\emph{Development pool and qualifying cohort:} Drawn from NASA spacecraft archives \cite{Hundman2018b}, the University of California, Irvine (UCI) Machine Learning Repository \cite{bachlin2010wearable, asuncion2007uci}, TimeEval \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}, and mTSBench \cite{zhou2026mtsbench}, the 45-stream pool spans $K\in[2,72]$ channels across three domains:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Spacecraft and solar:} six SMAP ($K{=}25$) and six MSL ($K{=}55$) streams divided by NASA into out-of-range \emph{point} and in-range \emph{contextual} faults \cite{Hundman2018b}, plus SWAN-SF solar magnetograms \cite{angryk2020multivariate} ($K{=}38$).
    \item \emph{Wearables:} six $64\,\text{Hz}$ Daphnet Freezing-of-Gait (FoG) streams \cite{bachlin2010wearable} ($K{=}9$, $72$ episodes, where $S{=}64$ spans $1\,\text{s}$).
    \item \emph{Industrial and infrastructure:} 14 slow-ramp Gasoil Heating Loop (GHL) streams ($K{=}16$, $26$ faults), Genesis ($K{=}18$), GECCO ($K{=}9$), Metro I-94 ($K{=}5$), Room-Occupancy \cite{candanedo2016accurate} ($K{=}5$), CalIt2 \cite{asuncion2007uci} ($K{=}2$), and six CICIDS streams ($K{=}72$).
\end{enumerate}
Thirty-three qualifying streams ($19$ non-GHL and $14$ GHL across $7$ datasets, $N_{\text{cal}} \ge 500$, $K\le 55$, $\le 1.2{\times}10^5$ test steps) form the primary dev cohort (Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(c)), while short or $K{=}72$ streams stay aside for archive tests.

\noindent\emph{Evaluation archive:} The 141-stream archive spans 12 datasets ($\ge 100$ calibration windows and one fault; $1{,}920\text{--}132{,}481$ healthy steps): SMAP ($47$), Daphnet ($22$), MSL ($18$), SMD ($18$), Exathlon ($17$), OPPORTUNITY ($13$), plus single-stream GECCO, Genesis, Pooled Server Metrics (PSM), CreditCard, Metro, and SWAN. Excluding the $23$ tuning streams leaves $118$ untouched streams across nine sets ($37$ zero-tuning external streams, $1$ GECCO stream, and $80$ unseen streams from tuned sets; Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}(b)).

\noindent\emph{Per-stream fitting and caveats:} Each stream fits only on its own nominal prefix, transferring the frozen architecture, damping, tripwire percentile, and quorum across $K\in[2,55]$ without cross-asset weights (Section~\ref{subsec:exp_setup}). Because SMAP and MSL contain labeling flaws \cite{wu2023flawed} and same-facility streams share hardware, evaluations combine dataset-clustered inference ($7$ dev, $9$ archive sets), nested leave-one-dataset-out (LODO) tuning, and 9-fold exclusion sensitivity (Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}).
