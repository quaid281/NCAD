\section{Multi-Sensor Internet-of-Things and Remote Sensing Benchmark Suite}
\label{sec:dataset}

Our benchmark suite comprises 11 datasets spanning 45 continuous telemetry streams from NASA spacecraft archives \cite{Hundman2018b}, the University of California, Irvine (UCI) Machine Learning Repository \cite{bachlin2010wearable, asuncion2007uci}, TimeEval \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}, and mTSBench \cite{zhou2026mtsbench}. Thirty-three streams enter the primary multi-seed held-out protocol; 12 are reserved with explicit accounting below. The suite spans four physical domains and separates natural operational or physiological anomalies from testbed or simulated faults. \emph{Spacecraft and solar telemetry} covers six SMAP streams ($K{=}25$ channels), six MSL rover streams ($K{=}55$, four primary and two short-nominal), and SWAN-SF solar magnetograms \cite{angryk2020multivariate} ($K{=}38$), all containing natural operational or solar-flare events. \emph{Wearable kinematics} covers six Daphnet Freezing-of-Gait (FoG) streams \cite{bachlin2010wearable} sampled at $64\,\text{Hz}$ ($K{=}9$, $72$ natural gait-freezing episodes, where $S{=}64$ equals a $1\,\text{s}$ horizon). \emph{Industrial processes and smart infrastructure} covers 14 Gasoil Heating Loop (GHL) streams ($K{=}16$, $26$ testbed valve and heater faults), the Genesis pick-and-place testbed ($K{=}18$), GECCO water chemistry at $1$-minute resolution ($K{=}9$, $37$ natural hydrological anomalies), Metro I-94 hourly highway traffic ($K{=}5$), Room-Occupancy sensing \cite{candanedo2016accurate} ($K{=}5$), CalIt2 building flow \cite{asuncion2007uci} ($K{=}2$), and six CICIDS network streams ($K{=}72$, simulated attack flows). Cohort~3 (Section~\ref{subsec:gating_ablation}) separately injects six controlled single-channel faults into six real streams.

Each stream provides nominal training sequences ($N_{\text{tr}} \in [2{,}074, 120{,}000]$ across the 33 primary streams) and labeled anomaly test sequences ($N_{\text{test}} \in [2{,}158, 114{,}001]$). Channels are standardized by training statistics with $\bm{\sigma}$ floored at $10^{-2}$ against flatlines, matching standardized archive subsets in prior benchmarks \cite{Hundman2018b, schmidl2022anomaly}.

Each nominal sequence is split chronologically into training ($65\%$), nominal calibration ($17.5\%$), and held-out nominal evaluation ($17.5\%$) blocks separated by an unobserved 64-step isolation gap (Section~\ref{subsec:exp_setup}). Inclusion in the primary multi-seed protocol required two prespecified criteria: (i)~at least $N_{\text{cal}} \ge 500$ calibration windows (requiring $T_{\text{nom}} \ge 3{,}769$), and (ii)~tractable stride-1 multi-seed runtime ($K \le 55$, $N_{\text{test}} \le 1.2\times 10^5$). Exactly 33 streams across seven datasets satisfy both criteria (Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(c)). Among the 12 reserved streams, five have nominal sequences too short ($T_{\text{nom}} \in [764, 1{,}492]$) to yield the required $500$ calibration windows ($N_{\text{cal}} \le 101$ across CalIt2, MSL C-2, MSL D-16, and two Room-Occupancy streams). Six CICIDS network streams ($K{=}72, N_{\text{test}} \ge 1.36\times 10^5$) exceed the stride-1 multi-seed runtime envelope, and GECCO water-quality ($K{=}9$, $1$ held-out event) is used in Cohorts 3--4. All 11 datasets and 130 archive streams appear in the secondary single-seed cross-check (Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(d)).

Two caveats bound external validity: (i)~SMAP and MSL contain documented labeling flaws \cite{wu2023flawed}; and (ii)~streams within SMAP, MSL, Daphnet, and GHL share hardware platforms ($N{=}7$ dataset clusters). We therefore report Dataset-Macro means, between-stream and within-stream seed standard deviations (SDs), zero-tuning leave-one-dataset-out (LODO) sensitivity, and dataset-clustered permutation tests (Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(c)).
