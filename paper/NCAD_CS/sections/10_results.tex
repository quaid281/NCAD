\section{Results and Discussion}
\label{sec:results}

This section presents quantitative and qualitative analysis of NCAD-CS v5 across diverse multi-sensor benchmarks.

\subsection{Quantitative Performance}
As detailed in Table~\ref{tab:multidataset_benchmark}, NCAD-CS v5 improves macro-average PA-F1 from $0.2604$ to $\mathbf{0.3631}$ across 23 multi-dataset benchmark channels (a relative improvement of $+39.4\%$). Simultaneously, the spatial-temporal graph attention architecture elevates the theoretical representation ceiling (Oracle PA-F1) from $0.3917$ to $\mathbf{0.5975}$ ($+52.5\%$).

\subsection{Resolution of Relational Breakdown and Threshold Collapse}
Two primary failure modes previously constrained unsupervised time-series anomaly detection:
\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep, topsep=0pt, leftmargin=*]
    \item \textit{Cross-Sensor Relational Breakdown:} In high-dimensional sensor environments (e.g., OPPORTUNITY with 77 channels), anomalous states frequently manifest as desynchronization between correlated sensors. The multi-head \texttt{RelationalGATEncoder} captures these dynamic inter-variable topologies, raising macro PA-F1 from $0.1253$ to $0.1834$ (and up to $0.4045$ on individual activity recognition streams).
    \item \textit{Zero-F1 Threshold Collapse:} Ad-hoc empirical elbow heuristics often selected overly conservative thresholds, leading to zero true positives on low-contrast anomalies. Extreme Value Theory (EVT / SPOT) calibration establishes a mathematically grounded tail cutoff, successfully recovering previously failing channels (e.g. Daphnet \texttt{S03R01E1} and Room-Occupancy \texttt{default}) with robust F1 scores above $0.30$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Limitations and Future Horizons}
While NCAD-CS v5 exhibits strong generalization, memory scaling with high sampling frequencies ($>100\text{Hz}$) remains an active area of optimization. Integrating non-contrastive Joint Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPA) and online streaming memory eviction policies represents a promising direction for future work.
