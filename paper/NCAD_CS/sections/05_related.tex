\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\noindent\emph{Observation-Space Forecasting and Reconstructive Detectors:} Deep detectors typically reconstruct windows and threshold $\|\mathbf{X} - \hat{\mathbf{X}}\|_F^2$. Long short-term memory (LSTM) forecasters \cite{Hundman2018b}, TranAD \cite{tuli2022tranad}, Anomaly Transformer \cite{xu2022anomaly}, and Fast Fourier Transform (FFT)-period TimesNet \cite{wu2023timesnet} fit raw jitter, causing heavy-tailed (\emph{leptokurtic}, $\gamma_2 > 0$) channel-sum spikes across $K \ge 25$ channels under baseline shift \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}.

\noindent\emph{Contrastive and Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures:} Contrastive encoders \cite{zhang2023self} including temporal-contextual contrasting \cite{eldele2021time}, TS2Vec \cite{yue2022ts2vec}, Neural Contextual Anomaly Detection (NCAD) \cite{carmona2021neural}, and DCdetector \cite{yang2023dcdetector} rely on synthetic perturbations matching test faults (Section~\ref{subsec:core_benchmark}). Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) \cite{lecun2022path, assran2023self, bardes2024vjepa} predict target latents $z_{\text{tgt}}$ from context $z_{\text{ctx}}$ without decoding, avoiding collapse via exponential moving average (EMA) targets \cite{grill2020bootstrap} or variance-covariance terms \cite{bardes2021vicreg, zbontar2021barlow, ermolov2021whitening}.

\noindent\emph{Residual Decomposition, Conformal Thresholding, and Statistical Process Control:} Telemetry reflects underlying system dynamics \cite{brunton2016discovering}. Neural ordinary differential equations (ODEs) \cite{chen2018neural}, Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) \cite{raissi2019physics}, Hamiltonian or Lagrangian nets \cite{greydanus2019hamiltonian, cranmer2020lagrangian}, and Koopman lifts \cite{koopman1931hamiltonian, mezic2005spectral, brunton2016koopman, lusch2018deep, liu2023koopa} learn physical laws. However, partial differential equation (PDE) penalties suffer stiff gradients on uncalibrated sensors \cite{wang2021understanding}, so we borrow Reynolds averaging \cite{reynolds1895dynamical} strictly as residual algebra. Classical statistical process control (SPC) charts such as Hotelling's $T^2$ \cite{hotelling1947multivariate} and Multivariate Exponentially Weighted Moving Average (MEWMA) \cite{lowry1992multivariate} collapse coordinates into one scalar. SPOT \cite{siffer2017anomaly} likewise fits one extreme-value \cite{balkema1974residual} Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD) tail across coupled steps \cite{ferro2003inference}. Coordinate Cumulative Sum (CUSUM) banks \cite{page1954continuous} and split-conformal Bonferroni, \v{S}id\'{a}k, or Benjamini--Hochberg (BH) false-discovery-rate (FDR) rules \cite{benjamini1995controlling} hit a discrete $(N_{\text{cal}}+1)^{-1}$ floor on short histories (Section~\ref{subsec:controlled_ablations}). Evaluations omit point adjustment \cite{kim2022towards, wu2023flawed, schmidl2022anomaly}.
