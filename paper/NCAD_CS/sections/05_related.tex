\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\noindent\emph{Observation-Space Forecasting and Reconstructive Detectors:} Deep time-series anomaly detectors predominantly forecast sensor readings or reconstruct input windows in raw observation space, flagging timestamps where point-level error $\|\mathbf{X} - \hat{\mathbf{X}}\|_F^2$ exceeds a threshold. Autoregressive long short-term memory (LSTM) networks with non-parametric dynamic thresholding \cite{Hundman2018b} model sequential dependencies directly on spacecraft channels. Recent Transformer and spectral reconstructors introduce specialized attention and frequency modules: TranAD \cite{tuli2022tranad} combines adversarial two-phase training with self-conditioning sub-series attention; Anomaly Transformer \cite{xu2022anomaly} contrasts prior Gaussian kernel associations against series-level self-attention; and TimesNet \cite{wu2023timesnet} folds 1D sequences into 2D tensors along dominant Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) periods for 2D convolutional modeling. Because point-level losses penalize error across every channel equally, reconstructors fit stochastic sensor jitter and environmental fluctuations. In high-dimensional streams ($K \ge 25$), summing raw channel errors accumulates heavy-tailed (\emph{leptokurtic}, $\gamma_2 > 0$) residual spikes that destabilize extreme-value threshold fitting under baseline shift \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}.

\noindent\emph{Contrastive and Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures:} Self-supervised representation methods map windows into a compact latent space \cite{zhang2023self}. Contrastive detectors construct positive and negative pairs: temporal and contextual contrasting \cite{eldele2021time} and TS2Vec \cite{yue2022ts2vec} maximize contextual agreement across hierarchical sub-series; Neural Contextual Anomaly Detection (NCAD) \cite{carmona2021neural} contrasts full-window and context-only embeddings using injected synthetic anomalies; and DCdetector \cite{yang2023dcdetector} employs dual patch-wise and in-patch attention without reconstruction. However, contrastive detectors depend on whether synthetic perturbations or patch discrepancies match test-time fault morphology ($0.058$ and $0.070$ macro Point-F1 for NCAD and DCdetector vs.\ $0.160$ for latent prediction across 11 datasets, Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(d)). Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) \cite{lecun2022path, assran2023self, bardes2024vjepa} eliminate observation decoding and negative pairs by encoding context $z_{\text{ctx}}$ and predicting the target embedding $z_{\text{tgt}}$ produced by a separate target encoder. Collapse is prevented via exponential moving average (EMA) target updates \cite{grill2020bootstrap} or variance-covariance penalties such as VICReg \cite{bardes2021vicreg}, Barlow Twins \cite{zbontar2021barlow}, and batch whitening \cite{ermolov2021whitening}. How latent predictive residuals behave under unsupervised extreme-value threshold calibration remains unexplored.

\noindent\emph{Fluid-Dynamic Reynolds Decomposition and Spectral Concentration Priors:} Multi-sensor telemetry reflects physical dynamical trajectories \cite{brunton2016discovering}. Neural ordinary differential equations (ODEs) \cite{chen2018neural}, Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) \cite{raissi2019physics}, and Hamiltonian or Lagrangian networks \cite{greydanus2019hamiltonian, cranmer2020lagrangian} enforce differential conservation laws on physical coordinates, but require known governing equations and suffer from stiff gradient pathologies on uncalibrated sensor arrays \cite{wang2021understanding}. Koopman and operator-theoretic models \cite{koopman1931hamiltonian, mezic2005spectral, brunton2016koopman, lusch2018deep, liu2023koopa} lift nonlinear dynamics into learned observable spaces. Rather than imposing partial differential equations on raw sensors, we draw on Reynolds decomposition \cite{reynolds1895dynamical} from turbulence modeling to separate multi-horizon latent predictions into a mean trajectory and centered sub-horizon fluctuations. We then measure wavepacket localization across prediction horizons via a Lieb--Thirring spectral concentration ratio \cite{lieb1976inequalities} on the centered fluctuation Gram matrix.

\noindent\emph{Extreme-Value Threshold Calibration and Evaluation Rigor:} Grounded in the Pickands--Balkema--de Haan extreme-value theorem \cite{balkema1974residual}, Streaming Peaks-Over-Threshold (SPOT) \cite{siffer2017anomaly} fits a Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD) to score exceedances above quantile $u$ to extrapolate a threshold at design risk $q$. However, continuous telemetry violates the classical independent and identically distributed (i.i.d.) assumption through short-range serial correlation (quantified by the Ferro--Segers extremal index \cite{ferro2003inference}) and inter-block regime drift. Because ``point-adjusted'' F1 severely inflates scores \cite{kim2022towards, wu2023flawed, schmidl2022anomaly}, we evaluate all models using unadjusted Point-F1, PR-AUC, causal vs.\ buffered attribution, and matched realized false-alarm operating curves.
