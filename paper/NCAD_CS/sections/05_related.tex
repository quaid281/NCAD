\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\noindent\emph{Observation-Space Forecasting and Reconstructive Detectors:} Deep detectors typically forecast or reconstruct raw windows and threshold $\|\mathbf{X} - \hat{\mathbf{X}}\|_F^2$. Autoregressive long short-term memory (LSTM) forecasters \cite{Hundman2018b} model spacecraft channels directly, TranAD \cite{tuli2022tranad} pairs adversarial training with self-conditioning attention, Anomaly Transformer \cite{xu2022anomaly} contrasts prior and series attention, and TimesNet \cite{wu2023timesnet} reshapes 1D series along Fast Fourier Transform (FFT) periods for 2D convolution. Equal channel losses fit jitter, so summing across $K \ge 25$ channels yields heavy-tailed (\emph{leptokurtic}, $\gamma_2 > 0$) spikes under baseline shift \cite{schmidl2022anomaly}.

\noindent\emph{Contrastive and Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures:} Representation methods encode windows into compact latents \cite{zhang2023self}. Contrastive methods such as temporal-contextual contrasting \cite{eldele2021time}, TS2Vec \cite{yue2022ts2vec}, synthetic-anomaly Neural Contextual Anomaly Detection (NCAD) \cite{carmona2021neural}, and dual-attention DCdetector \cite{yang2023dcdetector} depend on whether synthetic perturbations or patch discrepancies match test faults (Section~\ref{subsec:core_benchmark}). Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) \cite{lecun2022path, assran2023self, bardes2024vjepa} predict target embeddings $z_{\text{tgt}}$ from context $z_{\text{ctx}}$ without decoding or negative pairs, preventing collapse via exponential moving average (EMA) targets \cite{grill2020bootstrap} or variance-covariance terms \cite{bardes2021vicreg, zbontar2021barlow, ermolov2021whitening}.

\noindent\emph{Residual Decomposition, Conformal Thresholding, and Statistical Process Control:} Telemetry reflects system dynamics \cite{brunton2016discovering}. Neural ordinary differential equations (ODEs) \cite{chen2018neural}, Physics-Informed Neural Networks (PINNs) \cite{raissi2019physics}, Hamiltonian/Lagrangian nets \cite{greydanus2019hamiltonian, cranmer2020lagrangian}, and Koopman lifts \cite{koopman1931hamiltonian, mezic2005spectral, brunton2016koopman, lusch2018deep, liu2023koopa} model structured laws, though partial differential equation (PDE) penalties exhibit stiff gradients on uncalibrated sensors \cite{wang2021understanding}. We borrow the mean-plus-fluctuation split of Reynolds averaging \cite{reynolds1895dynamical} purely as residual algebra. Multivariate statistical process control (SPC) charts such as Hotelling's $T^2$ \cite{hotelling1947multivariate} and Multivariate Exponentially Weighted Moving Average (MEWMA) \cite{lowry1992multivariate} collapse coordinates into one scalar, as do 1D deep scores calibrated via extreme-value theory \cite{balkema1974residual} and Generalized Pareto Distribution (GPD) tails in SPOT \cite{siffer2017anomaly} under serial dependence \cite{ferro2003inference}. Per-coordinate Cumulative Sum (CUSUM) banks \cite{page1954continuous} and split-conformal rules under Bonferroni, \v{S}id\'{a}k, or Benjamini--Hochberg (BH) false-discovery-rate (FDR) control \cite{benjamini1995controlling} hit a discrete $(N_{\text{cal}}+1)^{-1}$ floor on short histories (Section~\ref{subsec:controlled_ablations}). All evaluations omit point adjustment \cite{kim2022towards, wu2023flawed, schmidl2022anomaly}.
