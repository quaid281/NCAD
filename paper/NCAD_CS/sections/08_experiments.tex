\section{Experimental Protocol and Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup and Calibration Protocol}
\label{subsec:exp_setup}
All architectures use context $C=256$, target horizon $S=64$ ($W=320$, or $S \in \{1, 4, 16, 32, 64\}$ in Section~\ref{subsec:horizon_latency}), latent dimension $D=32$, nominal training stride $16$, and evaluation stride $1$. Models are trained with AdamW \cite{loshchilov2017decoupled} (learning rate $10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$) and zero per-dataset tuning. Development cohorts train at batch $16$ for $15\text{--}20$ fixed epochs, while archive cohorts train at batch $16\text{--}64$ for up to $12$ early-stopped epochs with gradient clipping ($1.0$) and cosine annealing.

\paragraph{Disjoint Calibration Protocol}
Each stream's nominal history $\mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}} \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{nom}} \times K}$ is split chronologically (Fig.~\ref{fig:protocol_and_permutation}) into a fitting prefix $\mathbf{X}_{\text{tr}} = \mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}}[0 : N_{\text{tr}})$ ($N_{\text{tr}} = T_{\text{nom}} - \lfloor 0.35\, T_{\text{nom}}\rfloor$) and a validation suffix $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}} = \mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}}[N_{\text{tr}} : T_{\text{nom}})$, with all weights and thresholds frozen on $\mathbf{X}_{\text{hold}}$ and $\mathbf{X}_{\text{test}}$:
(1)~\emph{Stage~A (Disjoint fitting, selection, and adaptation):} Reusing $\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$ for early stopping or stabilization (\ref{eq:stabilized_whitening}) can make thresholds optimistic. The primary 118/141-stream archive therefore uses a disjoint \emph{five-block protocol} ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{sel}}\perp\mathbf{X}_q\perp\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$). Specifically, $\mathbf{X}_{\text{fit}}$ ($\approx 43\%$ of $T_{\text{nom}}$) fits standardization, network weights, $\bar{\bm{\mu}}_{\text{ctx}}$, and $\mathbf{\Omega}\in\mathcal{S}_{+}^{MD}$; $\mathbf{X}_{\text{sel}}$ ($N_{\text{sel}}\ge 96$ windows) selects the checkpoint; and $S$-isolated $\mathbf{X}_q$ ($N_q\in[160,2{,}048]$ windows) fits and freezes (\ref{eq:stabilized_whitening}) before $\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$ is touched. Exploratory fixed-epoch suites omit $\mathbf{X}_{\text{sel}}$ ($65\%$ $\mathbf{X}_{\text{tr}}$, unblended on the 19-stream cache and 130-stream screen, or sharing $\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$ on the 33-stream suite). A disjoint four-block check ($50\%$ $\mathbf{X}_{\text{fit}}\to\mathbf{X}_q\to\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}\to\mathbf{X}_{\text{hold}}$) on the 19 development streams confirms matching causal $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ ($0.96\%$ for \emph{Lite}, $1.05\%$ for \emph{Laminar-JEPA}), though the shorter fitting prefix lowers causal F1 ($0.133\to 0.071$).
(2)~\emph{Stage~B (Untouched calibration and autocorrelation on $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}$):} Splitting the $M_{\text{val}} = N_{\text{val}} - W + 1$ stride-1 windows at $H = \lfloor M_{\text{val}} / 2 \rfloor$ defines $\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}=[0 : H)$ and $\mathbf{X}_{\text{hold}}=[H + S : M_{\text{val}})$, separated by an $S=64$-step gap so target steps never overlap. Widening this gap to $G=320$ shifts causal FPR by $<0.25$ points. Requiring $N_{\text{cal}}\ge 500$ ensures at least $500(1-0.995)=2.5$ raw tail windows above $q_{0.995}$, whereas serial dependence ($\tau_{\text{int}}$ up to $206$ via Sokal's $c=5$ estimator) shrinks effective sample size to $N_{\text{eff}} = N_{\text{cal}}/\tau_{\text{int}}$ (Section~\ref{subsec:calibration_realities}).

\paragraph{Statistical vs.\ Practical Significance}
Seeds are averaged per stream, with seed standard deviation (SD) $\le 0.008$ on archive causal F1 and $\le 1.9\%$ on ER, so the stream is the independent unit. We report both \emph{dataset-clustered inference}, using $95\%$ cluster-bootstrap confidence intervals (CIs) and exact $2^D$ dataset sign-flip tests, and stream-level paired Wilcoxon tests ($2{,}000$ bootstrap resamples). Benjamini--Hochberg (BH) correction \cite{benjamini1995controlling} is applied across the four primary 118-stream causal contrasts (\emph{Lite} vs.\ Multi-Horizon 1D and TS-JEPA on Point-F1 and ER), and we evaluate 12-fold leave-one-dataset-out (LODO) subsets alongside per-dataset breakdowns to separate statistical significance from practical effect size (Section~\ref{subsec:calibration_realities}).

\subsection{Cohorts, Baseline Fairness, and Reproducibility}
\label{subsec:cohort_repro}
Causal and buffered modes share identical splits per cohort:
(1)~\emph{Development cohorts (design-stage):} Because these streams guided design and the 33-stream suite shares $\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$ for stabilization and thresholds, they test architectural factors rather than out-of-sample calibration. The 19-stream cache across $6$ datasets tests the dial ladder and detector banks in Tables~\ref{tab:ablations}(a) and \ref{tab:matched_alert}, while the 33-stream suite ($19$ non-GHL plus $14$ GHL across $7$ datasets) compares external baselines in Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}. Hyperparameters were fixed once on $23$ tuning streams ($19$ non-GHL plus \texttt{SMAP T-2}, \texttt{MSL P-10}, \texttt{Daphnet S09R01E2}, \texttt{OPPORTUNITY S1-ADL3}).
(2)~\emph{Untouched archive (primary evidence):} Removing those $23$ streams from the $141$-stream archive ($12$ datasets) leaves $118$ untouched streams across $9$ datasets ($101$ non-Exathlon, $8$ datasets; Tables~\ref{tab:calibrator_comparison}--\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}).

\noindent\emph{Baseline fairness across three tiers:} (i)~\emph{Matched-backbone controls} include TS-JEPA, Multi-Horizon JEPA (1D), and \emph{Laminar-JEPA (w/o Quench)} (omitting Steps Q1--Q4). They share identical encoders, window budgets, and seed resets ($\{42,123,456\}$) to isolate scalar SPOT from the breaker panel. Following Wu and Keogh \cite{wu2023flawed}, we also test marginal-preserving cycle-swap and phase-desync anomalies at random interior onsets alongside Gaussian noise injection ($\sigma\in\{0.15,0.30\}$) against a $\max_c|x_c(t)|$ one-liner. (ii)~\emph{System-level aggregators} score the exact same frozen whitened residuals under matched $\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$ alarm rates (Tables~\ref{tab:matched_alert}, \ref{tab:calibrator_comparison}). (iii)~\emph{External baselines} span TCN Forecaster/Autoencoder, PCA Forecaster, Isolation Forest, TimesNet \cite{wu2023timesnet}, TranAD \cite{tuli2022tranad}, Anomaly Transformer \cite{xu2022anomaly}, NCAD \cite{carmona2021neural}, and DCdetector \cite{yang2023dcdetector}. All external models use their published reference configurations ($206\text{k}\text{--}649\text{k}$ parameters vs.\ $120\text{k}\text{--}129\text{k}$ trainable for \emph{Lite}), identical training-only scaling, $W=320$ windowing, matched epoch budgets, $64$-step gaps, and SPOT ($u=p_{98}, q=10^{-3}$) without per-dataset tuning. All runs used PyTorch 2.4 / CUDA 12.4 on an RTX 5090 (code: \url{https://github.com/quaid281/NCAD}).

\subsection{Metrics}
\label{subsec:eval_metrics}
Alarms $\hat{y}(\tau)\in\{0,1\}$ and scores $N_{\text{tot}}(\tau)$ are evaluated step by step without point adjustment \cite{kim2022towards} (\emph{causal} = live at $\tau$; \emph{buffered} = retrospective review), averaging seeds per stream before cohort or dataset-macro ($\text{DS-F1}$) aggregation:
(1)~\emph{False alarms ($\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$, $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$):} healthy fraction flagged on $\mathbf{X}_{\text{hold}}$ and on test steps $y(\tau)=0$ (reported via mean, median, $\text{p90}$, and $\text{p95}$).
(2)~\emph{Step accuracy:} for Point-F1 and precision-recall (PR) AUC without point adjustment, Point-F1 is naturally $0.05\text{--}0.25$ because residuals peak near transitions and rare fault prevalence $\pi$ bounds trivial alarming at $\text{F1}_{\text{all}}=2\pi/(1+\pi)$; PR-AUC measures threshold-free ranking.
(3)~\emph{Episode coverage and speed:} ER is the fraction of contiguous $y=1$ runs hit at least once, compared against Bernoulli chance $\text{ER}_{\text{rand}}(p)$, while delay $\Delta t = t_{\text{alm}} - t^*$ measures steps from onset $t^*$ over detected episodes beyond the $S-1$ causal lag.
(4)~\emph{Operating controls and diagnostics:} \emph{Auto.}\ uses frozen healthy thresholds, $q^*=0.05$ uses $q_{0.95}$ on $\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$, and $f^*\in\{0.01,0.05\}$ matches test FPR post hoc as a ranking diagnostic. Excess kurtosis $\gamma_2$ measures $\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$ tail heaviness, while two-sample Kolmogorov--Smirnov (KS) distance $D_{\text{KS}}$ ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$ vs.\ $\mathbf{X}_{\text{hold}}$) measures nominal drift.
