\section{Experimental Protocol and Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Experimental Setup and Hyperparameters}
\label{subsec:exp_setup}
To ensure rigorous and fair comparison, all architectures are trained and evaluated under an identical streaming time-series pipeline. Telemetry streams are processed using a sliding window with past context duration $C=256$ and suspect target horizon $S=64$ (total window length $L=320$), evaluated with a stride of 1 timestep across all testing streams.

Context representations $z_{\text{ctx}} \in \mathbb{R}^D$ ($D=32$) are extracted via a multiscale dyadic front-end and causal dilated TCN (6 residual blocks, $F{=}48$, kernel $5$, dilations $\{1,2,4,8,16,32\}$); multi-statistic pooling (terminal, mean, max, and channel statistics) projects the $C{=}256$ context through a linear bottleneck recording pre-LayerNorm radial energy before LayerNorm and dropout ($p{=}0.20$). The momentum target encoder uses cosine EMA $m_{\text{base}}{=}0.996 \to m_{\text{max}}{=}0.9995$.

Models are optimized using AdamW with an initial learning rate $\eta=10^{-3}$ decayed to $\eta_{\text{min}}=10^{-5}$ via Cosine Annealing, weight decay $10^{-4}$, and mini-batch size $B=32$ across 20 training epochs on nominal telemetry sequences. 

\paragraph{Held-Out Calibration Protocol and Boundary Window Construction}
To evaluate out-of-sample threshold calibration without circularity, each stream's raw nominal sequence $\mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}} \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{nom}} \times K}$ is partitioned chronologically into a \emph{training block} $\mathbf{X}_{\text{tr}} = \mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}}[0 : N_{\text{tr}}]$ ($N_{\text{tr}} = \lfloor 0.65\, T_{\text{nom}}\rfloor$) and a disjoint \emph{held-out nominal block} $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}} = \mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}}[N_{\text{tr}} : T_{\text{nom}}]$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:protocol_and_permutation}(a)). Standardization ($\bm{\mu}, \bm{\sigma} \ge 10^{-2}$) and model weights ($E_\theta, E_\phi, \mathbf{K}$) are fit on $\mathbf{X}_{\text{tr}}$ windows ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{tr}}[j : j+W]$) alone, guaranteeing zero raw-observation or window overlap across $N_{\text{tr}}$. Within $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}$ ($N_{\text{val}} = T_{\text{nom}} - N_{\text{tr}}$), sliding windows of length $W = C + S = 320$ are constructed at stride $1$ strictly inside $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}$: window $i \in \{0, \dots, N_{\text{val}} - W\}$ uses context $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}[i : i+C]$ and target $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}[i+C : i+W]$. Under causal trailing attribution, the first $W - 1 = 319$ observations serve as context warm-up, yielding $M_{\text{val}} = N_{\text{val}} - W + 1$ valid score timestamps $v[0 : M_{\text{val}}]$ emitted at $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}[i + W - 1]$. Splitting $v$ at $H = \lfloor M_{\text{val}} / 2 \rfloor$ defines the \emph{nominal calibration block} $v_{\text{cal}} = v[0 : H]$ (fitting GPD parameters $\sigma, \xi$ above $u{=}p_{98}$ at $q{=}10^{-3}$ and $\gamma_2$) and the \emph{held-out evaluation block} $v_{\text{hold}} = v[H + S : M_{\text{val}}]$, excluding $S{=}64$ intermediate windows $v[H : H+S]$. Because suspect targets have length $S{=}64$, the last calibration target $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}[H-1+C : H-1+W]$ and first evaluation target $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}[H+S+C : H+S+W]$ are $100\%$ disjoint; the first $C{=}256$ evaluation steps share passive historical context $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}[H+S : H-1+W]$ with calibration observations, mirroring online streaming where past nominal history forms context for current predictions. A stricter full-window gap check ($G = W = 320$, excluding $v[H : H+320]$ so passive context is also disjoint across the seven streams with $M_{\text{val}}/2 > 420$) leaves stream-macro causal FPR virtually unchanged: $0.0207 \to 0.0218$ (OpEntropy) and $0.0302 \to 0.0324$ (TS-JEPA) vs.\ $0.1215 \to 0.1397$ (TimesNet) and $0.0905 \to 0.0973$ (TranAD). Thresholds remain fixed during test evaluation; dynamic rolling SPOT is evaluated in Section~\ref{subsec:causal_vs_buffered}.

\paragraph{Calibrator Assignment}
All experiments use standard SPOT ($u = p_{98}$, $q = 10^{-3}$, no kurtosis multiplier) on held-out nominal splits, except the rolling-SPOT incident analysis and the calibrator comparison itself (Table~\ref{tab:calibrator_comparison}); no table mixes calibrators across models.

\paragraph{Multi-Seed Replication, Primary Endpoints, and Equivalence Testing}
Principal models on the 19 non-GHL streams run five seeds (42, 123, 456, 7, 2024); the 14 GHL streams ($>10^6$ steps) and ablation controls run three seeds (five on the two smallest GHL streams). We report held-out nominal FPR, Point-F1, and PR-AUC as distinct outcomes; nonsignificant differences are not evidence of parity. Inference reports stream-macro means, seed SDs, moving-block bootstrap ($L{=}64$) per-cell FPR intervals, stream-resampled paired CIs (2{,}000 iterations, seeds averaged within stream, $n{=}19$ or $n{=}33$), dataset-clustered checks ($N{=}7$), and Benjamini--Hochberg FDR-adjusted Wilcoxon signed-rank tests ($q_{\text{FDR}}{=}0.05$). For physical regularizer ablations, we pair two-sided tests with Two One-Sided Tests (TOST): $H_{01}: \mu_1 - \mu_2 \le -\Delta$ ($p_{\text{lower}}$) and $H_{02}: \mu_1 - \mu_2 \ge +\Delta$ ($p_{\text{upper}}$; $p_{\text{TOST}} = \max(p_{\text{lower}}, p_{\text{upper}})$) alongside $90\%$ ($1{-}2\alpha$ at $\alpha{=}0.05$) and $95\%$ CIs. We prespecify $\Delta = \pm 0.03$ Point-F1 for fixed-backbone controls ($\approx 13\%$ of baseline F1, $<0.09\times$ between-stream SD) and $\Delta = \pm 0.05$ for cross-architecture contrasts ($\approx 25\%$ of baseline F1, $<0.15\times$ between-stream SD, corresponding at prevalence $\pi \approx 0.15$ to an event-recall shift of $\le 0.04$ or $\approx 15\text{--}20$ false alarms per $1{,}000$ nominal steps), and evaluate narrow-margin TOST sensitivity down to $\Delta \in \{\pm 0.010, \pm 0.015, \pm 0.020, \pm 0.030\}$ alongside TOST on held-out FPR and event recall (Section~\ref{subsec:controlled_ablations}). Equivalence requires $p_{\text{TOST}} < 0.05$ and the $90\%$ CI strictly inside $[-\Delta, +\Delta]$.

\subsection{Baseline Architectures, Fairness Checks, and Cohort Structure}
All models share a common streaming pipeline ($C{=}256, S{=}64$, stride $1$, AdamW $\eta{=}10^{-3} \to 10^{-5}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch $32$, $20$ epochs, SPOT $u{=}p_{98}, q{=}10^{-3}$). We separate primary multi-seed conclusions (Cohorts 1--2: 33-stream and 19-stream 5-seed benchmarks across 12 neural, linear, and non-parametric detectors including PCA, Isolation Forest, $k$-NN, MAD, and Z-score baselines) from secondary single-seed screens. Three fairness checks bound this protocol: (i)~\emph{Training-budget sensitivity} (7 streams $\times$ 3 seeds): doubling optimization from $10$ to $20$ epochs raises JEPA mean F1 from $0.038$ to $0.048$ (OpEntropy $0.029 \to 0.065$) while reconstructor F1 is stable ($0.158 \to 0.154$; TimesNet $0.154 \to 0.151$, TranAD $0.161 \to 0.158$) and held-out FPR stays $0.087\text{--}0.092$ vs.\ $0.333$. (ii)~\emph{Native published windows} (5 streams): at native windows (TranAD $W{=}10$, TimesNet $W{=}96$), F1 rises ($0.164$, $0.218$), but observation residuals remain leptokurtic ($\gamma_2 = 448.25$ and $98.14$ vs.\ $3.14$ for TS-JEPA) with high nominal FPR ($28.4\%$, $44.1\%$). (iii)~\emph{Published-score and secondary 130-stream single-seed cross-check}: baselines replicate published PA-F1 on SMD/MSL, and Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(d) provides a secondary 130-stream, 11-dataset single-seed screen against NCAD ($149{,}634$ parameters at $K{=}25$), DCdetector ($298{,}400$), and Anomaly Transformer ($4{,}785{,}120$).

\noindent\textbf{Cohort Summary and Reproducibility Specification:} Five cohorts address distinct questions: (1)~\emph{33-stream primary multi-seed suite} (19 non-GHL + 14 GHL, 3--5 seeds, Tables~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(a)--(c), \ref{tab:calibrator_comparison}(a)); (2)~\emph{19-stream non-GHL multi-seed mechanism/operating suite} (12 arms, 5 seeds, Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(a),(e), Table~\ref{tab:operating_and_horizon}(b), Fig.~\ref{fig:mechanism_operating_calibration}); (3)~\emph{6-stream factorial and fault suite} (3 seeds, Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(c),(d)); (4)~\emph{5-stream horizon and front-end panels} (3 seeds, Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(b), Table~\ref{tab:operating_and_horizon}(a)); and (5)~\emph{secondary 130-stream 11-dataset single-seed screen} (Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(d)). Parameter counts at $K{=}25$: OpEntropy $285{,}701$, TS-JEPA $289{,}061$, Reynolds $296{,}037$, Potential-Flow $338{,}209$, TimesNet $648{,}553$, TranAD $205{,}746$. Non-finite values are zero-imputed ($\bm{\sigma} \ge 10^{-2}$); SPOT fits GPD $(\xi, \sigma)$ above $u{=}p_{98}$ via Grimshaw MLE with L-BFGS-B fallback ($\xi \in [-0.9, 10], \sigma \ge 10^{-6}$). All 195 primary runs converged on PyTorch 2.4 / CUDA 12.4 (RTX 5090, $0.60$--$0.84$~ms/window, $183.8$--$185.3$~MB VRAM); manifests, thresholds, and code are at \url{https://github.com/quaid281/NCAD}.

\subsection{Multi-Tier Statistical Evaluation Metrics}
\label{subsec:eval_metrics}
To guard against point-adjustment inflation, channel-averaging bias, and threshold gaming \cite{schmidl2022anomaly, kim2022towards}, we report complementary evaluation tiers: (i)~\emph{operating characteristics}: event recall over deployable calibration quantiles ($q^*$) and matched realized-test-FPR budgets ($f^* \in [0.01,0.50]$, interpolated within stream--seed curves); and (ii)~\emph{secondary endpoints}: Dataset-Macro Point-F1 (unweighted across datasets so 14-stream GHL does not dominate), channel-macro Point-F1, PR-AUC (threshold-free average precision), event recall ($\ge 1$ alarm per contiguous anomaly segment), mean detection delay $\Delta t$ (onset-to-first-alarm lag over detected events), the constant-alarm reference $\text{F1}_{\text{all-pos}} = 2\pi/(1+\pi)$ at prevalence $\pi$, pooled micro-F1, and contiguous/test-sweep PA-F1.
