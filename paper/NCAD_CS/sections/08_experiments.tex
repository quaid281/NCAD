\section{Experimental Protocol and Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Setup and Disjoint Calibration Protocol}
\label{subsec:exp_setup}
Every architecture uses a $256$-step context, a $64$-step target ($W=320$, swept down to $S=1$ in Section~\ref{subsec:horizon_latency}), a $32$-dimensional latent space, stride-$16$ training, and stride-$1$ live evaluation. Models train with AdamW \cite{loshchilov2017decoupled} (learning rate $10^{-3}$, weight decay $10^{-4}$) and zero per-dataset hyperparameter tuning, using $15\text{--}20$ fixed epochs on development benchmarks and up to $12$ early-stopped epochs on the archive.

\paragraph{Five-Block Disjoint Calibration}
To guarantee zero threshold leakage, each stream's healthy history $\mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}}$ splits chronologically into five target-disjoint blocks separated by $64$-step isolation gaps (Algorithm~\ref{alg:pipeline}), freezing all weights and thresholds before test evaluation:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Model fitting and stabilization:} The first block ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{fit}}$, about $43\%$ of $\mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}}$ in the archive) fits channel scaling, encoder-predictor weights, and whitening $\mathbf{\Omega}^{1/2}$. A separate validation slice ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{sel}}$) chooses the early-stopped checkpoint, and a third slice ($\mathbf{X}_q$) freezes the whitening shrinkage weights (\ref{eq:stabilized_whitening}) before calibration data are ever touched.
    \item \emph{Threshold calibration and held-out screening:} The final $35\%$ of $\mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}}$ splits across a $64$-step gap into threshold calibration ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{cal}}$) and held-out screening ($\mathbf{X}_{\text{hold}}$). Widening all post-fit gaps from $64$ steps to a full $320$-step window across $49$ eligible streams shifts causal ER, Point-F1, and precision by at most $0.001$ (all $p \ge 0.66$), confirming negligible context bleed. Because overlapping sliding windows correlate strongly, a $500$-window calibration block contains a median of only $65$ effectively independent samples, making $N_{\text{cal}} \ge 500$ a practical operating rule rather than a formal tail guarantee (Section~\ref{subsec:finite_sample_calibration}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{Statistical Inference}
Random seeds are averaged within each stream first, with seed standard deviation $\le 0.008$ on causal F1 and $\le 1.9\%$ on ER, keeping each telemetry stream as the unit of analysis. Because streams from the same facility share hardware, we pair $95\%$ dataset-clustered bootstrap confidence intervals (CIs) with exact dataset sign-flip tests, alongside stream-level paired Wilcoxon tests with Benjamini--Hochberg (BH) control \cite{benjamini1995controlling} across the four primary contrasts.

\subsection{Cohorts, Baselines, and Provenance}
\label{subsec:cohort_repro}
Evaluations separate development from untouched testing across two cohorts. The \emph{development cohort} comprises the $19$-stream residual cache (Tables~\ref{tab:ablations}(a), \ref{tab:matched_alert}) and the $33$-stream primary benchmark (Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}). All detector hyperparameters freeze once on $23$ tuning streams ($19$ non-GHL dev streams plus four archive tuning streams), leaving $118$ untouched archive streams across nine datasets (Tables~\ref{tab:calibrator_comparison} and \ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}).

Baselines span matched-backbone controls and external detectors. \emph{Matched-backbone controls} test single-horizon TS-JEPA, Multi-Horizon 1D JEPA, alternative aggregators on frozen residuals, and Wu--Keogh cycle-swap, phase-desync, and noise checks against raw amplitude $\max_c|x_c(t)|$ \cite{wu2023flawed}. \emph{External baselines} cover TCN Forecaster/Autoencoder, Linear PCA Forecaster (LayerNorm), Isolation Forest, TimesNet \cite{wu2023timesnet}, TranAD \cite{tuli2022tranad}, Anomaly Transformer \cite{xu2022anomaly}, NCAD \cite{carmona2021neural}, and DCdetector \cite{yang2023dcdetector}. We run Linear PCA, TranAD, and TimesNet across all $118$ untouched streams (three seeds, SPOT $u=p_{98}, q=10^{-3}$), and release our code at \url{https://github.com/quaid281/NCAD}.

\paragraph{Design vs.\ Archive-Informed Policy}
To keep pre-specified design separate from archive-driven policy, choices fall into three tiers:
(1)~\emph{Frozen before first archive run:} the entire \emph{Laminar-JEPA-Lite} architecture, whitening settings, $N_{\text{cal}} \ge 500$ rule, per-dial $q_{0.985}$ tripwire, quorum (\ref{eq:panel_quorum}), and live drift trigger ($D_{\text{KS}} \ge 0.50$).
(2)~\emph{Post-hoc archive diagnostics} (frozen detector unchanged): block permutation, matched-budget comparisons, PCA/ZCA checks, $W=320$ gap checks, and multiplicative floors.
(3)~\emph{Archive-informed readiness screening:} pre-deployment held-out FPR ($\le 5\%$) and drift ($D_{\text{KS}} \le 0.50$) gates that switch short or drifting streams to 1D SPOT scalar calibration. Nine-fold nested LODO validates those gate thresholds on each fold's eight labeled training datasets while deploying strictly label-free on the held-out ninth dataset (Table~\ref{tab:dataset_lodo_audit}(a,c)).

\subsection{Evaluation Metrics}
\label{subsec:eval_metrics}
All predictions are scored step-by-step without point adjustment \cite{kim2022towards} and averaged across seeds before cohort or dataset-macro ($\text{DS-F1}$) aggregation. Four dimensions summarize operator-facing performance:
(1)~\emph{False-alarm burden:} pointwise held-out $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ and prospective $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$ (mean, median, p90, p95), plus contiguous false-alarm episodes per $1{,}000$ healthy test steps.
(2)~\emph{Stepwise accuracy and ranking:} precision, recall, Point-F1 (compared against the all-positive baseline $\text{F1}_{\text{all}}$), and precision-recall (PR) AUC.
(3)~\emph{Event coverage and detection speed:} event recall (ER, contrasted with random alarming $\text{ER}_{\text{rand}}(p)$) and onset latency $\Delta t = \tau_{\text{alm}} - t^* \ge 0$, reported both on shared co-detected fault segments (\emph{segment-paired}) and across streams where both methods trigger (\emph{stream-paired}).
(4)~\emph{Calibration diagnostics:} autonomous healthy thresholds (\emph{Auto.}) are contrasted against matched calibration ($q^*=0.05$) and post-hoc test FPR budgets ($f^* \in \{0.01, 0.05\}$), alongside calibration excess kurtosis $\gamma_2$ and Kolmogorov--Smirnov (KS) drift $D_{\text{KS}}$.
