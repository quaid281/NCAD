\section{Experimental Protocol and Evaluation Metrics}
\label{sec:exp}

\subsection{Experimental Setup and Hyperparameters}
\label{subsec:exp_setup}
All architectures use an identical streaming pipeline with context window $C=256$ and target horizon $S=64$ ($W = C + S = 320$), trained at stride $16$ and evaluated at stride $1$. Context and target representations ($D=32$) are extracted by the multiscale frequency front-end followed by a 6-block causal dilated TCN (64 filters, kernel size $5$, dilations $\{1,2,4,8,16,32\}$). Multi-statistic temporal pooling (terminal, mean, max, standard deviation, Log-Ricci energy, and first-order total variation) projects features through a linear bottleneck before LayerNorm and dropout ($p=0.20$). Models are trained for $20$ epochs on nominal sequences using AdamW \cite{loshchilov2017decoupled} (learning rate $10^{-3} \to 10^{-5}$ via cosine annealing, weight decay $10^{-4}$, batch size $32$), with identical training budgets and zero per-dataset hyperparameter tuning across families.

\paragraph{Held-Out Calibration Protocol and Boundary Isolation}
To evaluate out-of-sample threshold calibration without circularity, each stream's raw nominal sequence $\mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}} \in \mathbb{R}^{T_{\text{nom}} \times K}$ is partitioned chronologically (Fig.~\ref{fig:protocol_and_permutation}, Row~1) into a \emph{training block} $\mathbf{X}_{\text{tr}} = \mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}}[0 : N_{\text{tr}})$ ($N_{\text{tr}} = \lfloor 0.65\, T_{\text{nom}}\rfloor$) and a disjoint \emph{held-out nominal block} $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}} = \mathbf{X}_{\text{nom}}[N_{\text{tr}} : T_{\text{nom}})$. Standardization parameters and neural weights are fit on $\mathbf{X}_{\text{tr}}$ windows alone, guaranteeing zero raw-observation or window overlap across $N_{\text{tr}}$.

Within $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}$ ($N_{\text{val}} = T_{\text{nom}} - N_{\text{tr}}$), sliding windows of length $W = 320$ are constructed strictly inside $\mathbf{X}_{\text{val}}$ (Fig.~\ref{fig:protocol_and_permutation}, Row~2). Under causal trailing attribution, the first $W - 1 = 319$ observations provide context warm-up, yielding $M_{\text{val}} = N_{\text{val}} - W + 1$ valid score timestamps $v[0 : M_{\text{val}})$. Splitting $v$ at midpoint $H = \lfloor M_{\text{val}} / 2 \rfloor$ defines the \emph{nominal calibration block} $v_{\text{cal}} = v[0 : H)$ (used to fit the SPOT threshold at $u=p_{98}, q=10^{-3}$) and the \emph{held-out evaluation block} $v_{\text{hold}} = v[H + S : M_{\text{val}})$, excluding $S=64$ intermediate windows $v[H : H+S)$. Because suspect target windows have length $S=64$, the last calibration target and first evaluation target are $100\%$ disjoint. The first $C=256$ evaluation steps share passive historical context with calibration observations, mirroring online streaming where past nominal history forms context for current predictions. A full-window isolation check ($G = W = 320$, excluding $v[H : H+320)$ across the seven streams with $M_{\text{val}}/2 > 420$) confirms that shared passive context does not bias calibration: stream-macro causal FPR shifts only from $0.33\%$ to $0.36\%$ for Soliton-Reynolds JEPA and $3.02\%$ to $3.24\%$ for TS-JEPA, versus $12.15\%$ to $13.97\%$ for TimesNet and $9.05\%$ to $9.73\%$ for TranAD. All primary tables use fixed standard SPOT thresholds during testing.

\paragraph{Independent Statistical Units, Multi-Seed Replication, and Equivalence Bounds}
Principal models on the 19 non-GHL streams are evaluated across five random seeds ($\{42, 123, 456, 7, 2024\}$); the 14 GHL streams ($>10^6$ observations each) and ablation controls use three seeds ($\{7, 42, 2024\}$). To prevent pseudoreplication, seeds are averaged within each stream first ($\text{SD}_{\text{seed}}$), stream-level paired tests treat the telemetry stream ($n=33$ primary or $n=19$ non-GHL) as the unit of analysis, and domain-level tests treat the dataset family ($N=7$) as the independent cluster:
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Paired Wilcoxon signed-rank test and false-discovery-rate adjustment:} The Wilcoxon signed-rank test evaluates whether the median paired difference across streams is zero without Gaussian assumptions. We adjust $p$-values across multiple comparisons via the Benjamini--Hochberg false-discovery-rate (FDR) procedure \cite{benjamini1995controlling} ($q_{\text{FDR}} = 0.05$), report $95\%$ stream-resampled bootstrap confidence intervals (CIs; $2{,}000$ resamples), and cross-check at the dataset-cluster level ($N=7$) via exact sign-flip permutation tests and zero-tuning LODO sensitivity.
    \item \emph{Two One-Sided Tests (TOST) for fixed-backbone regularizer equivalence:} We use TOST \cite{schuirmann1987comparison} to test whether the multiplicative soliton and manifold gain in (\ref{eq:soliton_score}) alters aggregate Point-F1 relative to the unmodulated multi-horizon whitened envelope $\mathcal{S}_{\text{env}}(t)$ on the identical trained backbone ($n=19$ streams). TOST tests $H_{01}: \mu_1 - \mu_2 \le -\Delta$ ($p_{\text{lower}}$) and $H_{02}: \mu_1 - \mu_2 \ge +\Delta$ ($p_{\text{upper}}$), establishing equivalence when $p_{\text{TOST}} = \max(p_{\text{lower}}, p_{\text{upper}}) < 0.05$ across $\Delta \in \{\pm 0.015, \pm 0.020, \pm 0.030\}$ (Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(a)).
    \item \emph{Distributional tail and drift diagnostics ($\gamma_2$ and $D_{\text{KS}}$):} On nominal windows we measure: (i)~\emph{excess kurtosis} $\gamma_2 = \mathbb{E}[(s - \mu)^4]/\sigma^4 - 3$, distinguishing heavy-tailed \emph{leptokurtic} ($\gamma_2 > 0$) from light-tailed \emph{platykurtic} ($\gamma_2 < 0$) residuals; and (ii)~the two-sample \emph{Kolmogorov--Smirnov (KS) drift statistic} $D_{\text{KS}} = \sup_x |F_{\text{cal}}(x) - F_{\text{hold}}(x)| \in [0, 1]$, quantifying distributional shift between calibration ($F_{\text{cal}}$) and held-out nominal ($F_{\text{hold}}$) blocks.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Baseline Fairness Checks, Cohort Structure, and Reproducibility}
\label{subsec:cohort_repro}
Two fairness audits verify that reconstructive baseline results under the unified $20$-epoch, $W=320$ pipeline are not artifacts of window length or implementation: (i)~evaluating TranAD ($W=10$) and TimesNet ($W=96$) at their native published window sizes across five streams raises Point-F1 ($0.164$ and $0.218$), but observation residuals remain severely leptokurtic ($\gamma_2 = 448.3$ and $98.1$ vs.\ $3.1$ for TS-JEPA) with high nominal FPR ($28.4\%$ and $44.1\%$); and (ii)~reconstructors replicate their published point-adjusted F1 on SMD and MSL, while Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(d) provides a secondary 130-stream, 11-dataset single-seed cross-check against NCAD, DCdetector, and Anomaly Transformer.

Experiments span five cohorts: \emph{Cohort~1} ($33$ primary streams: $19$ non-GHL at $5$ seeds and $14$ GHL at $3$ seeds, Tables~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(a)--(c) and~\ref{tab:calibrator_comparison}(a)); \emph{Cohort~2} ($19$-stream, $12$-arm matched-backbone factorial and operating-curve suite at $5$ seeds, Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(a),(c) and Fig.~\ref{fig:operating_and_horizon}(b)); \emph{Cohort~3} ($6$-stream single-channel quiet-fault and hybrid-monitor suite at $3$ seeds, Fig.~\ref{fig:regularizer_and_quiet_fault}); \emph{Cohort~4} ($5$-stream horizon-latency sweep across $S \in \{1,4,16,32,64\}$ and dyadic front-end ablation at $3$ seeds, Fig.~\ref{fig:operating_and_horizon}(a) and Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(b)); and \emph{Cohort~5} ($130$-stream single-seed baseline screen across $11$ datasets, Table~\ref{tab:core_benchmark}(d)). At $K=25$ channels, online trainable parameter counts are closely matched: $272{,}266$ online parameters for TS-JEPA ($540{,}148$ total including the stop-gradient EMA target encoder), $311{,}658$ online ($618{,}892$ total) for Soliton-Reynolds JEPA, $304{,}138$ for the matched TCN observation forecaster, $307{,}402$ for its LayerNorm-bounded variant, $648{,}553$ for TimesNet, and $205{,}746$ for TranAD. Non-finite telemetry entries are zero-imputed with channel standard deviations floored at $10^{-2}$, and causal and buffered scores receive separately fitted thresholds $\tau_{\text{SPOT}}$ within their respective calibration windows $v_{\text{cal}}$ (\ref{eq:spot_threshold}). All runs converged under the $20$-epoch schedule on PyTorch 2.4 / CUDA 12.4 (NVIDIA RTX 5090, $0.60\text{--}0.88\,\text{ms}$ per window, $188\,\text{MB}$ GPU memory); code and manifests are at \url{https://github.com/quaid281/NCAD}.

\subsection{Multi-Tier Statistical Evaluation Metrics}
\label{subsec:eval_metrics}
To guard against point-adjustment inflation, channel-averaging bias, and threshold gaming \cite{schmidl2022anomaly, kim2022towards}, we evaluate models across three tiers. First, \emph{primary calibration and accuracy outcomes} comprise realized held-out nominal FPR ($\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$), out-of-sample calibration error $|\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}} - q|$ relative to design risk $q = 10^{-3}$, unadjusted Point-F1, and threshold-free PR-AUC. Second, \emph{operating characteristics} evaluate event recall across deployable calibration quantiles ($q^*$, Fig.~\ref{fig:operating_and_horizon}(b)) and across post-hoc matched realized test-FPR budgets ($f^* \in \{0.01, 0.05\}$, Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(c)). Third, \emph{secondary operational endpoints} report unweighted Dataset-Macro Point-F1, event recall (fraction of contiguous anomaly segments with $\ge 1$ alarm), mean detection delay $\Delta t$ over detected events, and the constant-alarm baseline $\text{F1}_{\text{all-pos}} = 2\pi/(1+\pi)$ at prevalence $\pi$.
