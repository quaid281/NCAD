\begin{abstract}
Deep reconstructive detectors for multivariate time-series anomaly detection minimize observation-space error, fitting stochastic sensor noise that produces heavy-tailed residuals and severe false-alarm flooding under automated threshold calibration. We investigate how Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) condition predictive residuals in a 32-dimensional latent space under distribution shift, comparing an unconstrained single-horizon baseline (TS-JEPA) against Soliton-Reynolds JEPA, a multi-horizon fluid-dynamic decomposition architecture that taps intermediate temporal convolutional stages, subtracts online context batch barycenters, whitens multi-horizon residuals, and modulates anomaly scores via a Lieb--Thirring spectral concentration ratio on centered sub-horizon fluctuations. Across 33 continuous telemetry streams from seven physical datasets (19 non-Gasoil Heating Loop (GHL) streams at five seeds and 14 GHL streams at three seeds) under chronological held-out Streaming Peaks-Over-Threshold (SPOT) calibration, we evaluate stream-macro realized false-positive rates (FPRs), out-of-sample calibration error from the 0.1\% design risk, point F1, and precision-recall area under the curve as primary outcomes. Latent scoring lowers unweighted stream-macro held-out FPRs three-fold under retrospective buffered attribution (9.3\% to 10.8\% for TS-JEPA and Soliton-Reynolds JEPA versus 30.0\% to 30.7\% for TimesNet and TranAD across the 19 non-GHL streams; 5.5\% to 6.3\% versus 19.6\% to 19.9\% stream-macro across all 33 streams) and ten- to eighty-fold under strictly causal streaming (2.4\% for TS-JEPA and 0.3\% for Soliton-Reynolds JEPA versus 22.2\% to 24.3\% across 19 non-GHL streams; 1.5\% and 0.3\% versus 14.2\% to 16.1\% across all 33 streams), while Soliton-Reynolds JEPA achieves leading buffered precision-recall area under the curve (0.375) and matched-FPR event recall (0.701 causal at 1\% FPR). Matched-encoder factorial ablations show that Layer Normalization (LayerNorm) bottlenecks compress residual kurtosis, whereas learned latent projections and multi-horizon barycenter whitening cut calibration-to-holdout drift, short prediction horizons (one to four steps) eliminate structural emission latency, and hybrid sensor variance monitors recover quiet single-channel faults at a quantified false-alarm cost.
\end{abstract}
