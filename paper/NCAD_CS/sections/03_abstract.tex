\begin{abstract}
Deep reconstructive detectors for multivariate time-series anomaly detection minimize observation-space error, fitting stochastic sensor noise that produces heavy-tailed residuals and severe false-alarm flooding under automated threshold calibration. We investigate Joint-Embedding Predictive Architectures (JEPAs) scoring predictive discrepancies in a low-dimensional latent space, alongside an Operator-Entropy variant (OpEntropy JEPA) whose linear Koopman operator incorporates spectral entropy and a shifted logarithmic Bregman barrier, coordinate saliency gating, and a multiscale dyadic frequency front-end. Across 33 continuous telemetry streams from seven physical datasets under chronological held-out Streaming Peaks-Over-Threshold calibration, latent predictive scoring improves relative false-alarm control three- to four-fold under retrospective buffered attribution (7.4\% to 10.1\% nominal false-positive rate versus 30.0\% for TimesNet and 30.7\% for TranAD) and eight- to twenty-fold under strictly causal streaming (1.1\% to 2.8\% versus 22.2\% to 24.3\%). This false-alarm suppression does not establish general detection superiority: point F1, precision-recall ranking, and event recall remain comparable to reconstructive baselines. Controlled mechanism ablations isolate two drivers of residual conditioning: low-dimensional LayerNorm bottlenecks compress residual kurtosis, while learned latent projections reduce inter-block distribution drift relative to normalized observation forecasters. On a fixed Koopman backbone, factorial and Two One-Sided Tests ablations confirm that spectral entropy and barrier terms preserve full operator rank without altering detection within a 0.03 F1 margin, whereas cross-architecture F1 differences against unconstrained TS-JEPA remain inconclusive despite shorter detection latency. Static extreme-value calibration still overshoots its 0.1\% design risk under deployment shift; a three-stage permutation decomposition attributes 57\% to 64\% of excess causal overshoot in latent models to inter-block drift and 29\% to 35\% to serial dependence.
\end{abstract}
