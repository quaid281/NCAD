\begin{abstract}
Unsupervised anomaly detection in complex multi-sensor time series is a critical capability across aerospace, cyber-physical infrastructure, industrial IoT, and digital health. However, prevailing paradigms suffer from fundamental dilemmas: symbolic equation discovery (e.g., SINDy) fails on noisy, partially-observed, high-dimensional telemetry; reconstructive deep models (e.g., Autoencoders, Masked Transformers) over-allocate capacity memorizing high-frequency stochastic sensor jitter; and deterministic predictors collapse to the unphysical conditional mean on multi-branching operational regimes. To address these challenges, we introduce \textbf{Flow-JEPA} (Time-Series Flow-Matching Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture), a continuous self-supervised framework that reformulates anomaly detection as tangent velocity vector field modeling on a regularized latent dynamical manifold. Flow-JEPA couples a causal context encoder with an Exponential Moving Average (EMA) momentum target encoder, training a continuous velocity network via \textbf{Optimal Transport Conditional Flow Matching (OT-CFM)} regularized by non-contrastive VICReg (Variance hinge, Covariance decorrelation) to strictly prevent dimensional collapse without synthetic negative sampling. To achieve zero-variance anomaly scoring at test time, we derive a \textbf{Deterministic Midpoint Velocity Discrepancy} evaluated at $t=0.5$, whitened by an empirical Mahalanobis precision tensor and asymptotically thresholded via \textbf{Extreme Value Theory (EVT / SPOT)} under the Pickands-Balkema-de Haan theorem. Comprehensive multi-domain benchmarking across 1,300 model-channel evaluations on 11 real-world datasets (NASA SMAP/MSL spacecraft telemetry, Daphnet Parkinsonian gait dynamics, GHL separation plant, cicids network intrusion, SWAN, room-occupancy IoT, GECCO, Genesis, CalIt2, and metro) demonstrates that Flow-JEPA consistently surpasses published SOTA baselines (TimesNet, TranAD, Anomaly Transformer, DCdetector, and NCAD), achieving a grand macro Point-F1 of $\mathbf{0.2389}$ (+52.5\% over TimesNet and +56.2\% over TranAD). Phase-space dynamical analyses confirm that Flow-JEPA models nominal physical dynamics as stable limit cycles, providing clear geometric and physical interpretability for anomaly breakout.
\end{abstract}
