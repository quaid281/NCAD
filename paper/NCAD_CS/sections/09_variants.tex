\subsection{Ablation 1: Dial Views, Single-Factor \emph{Lite} Isolation, and Quenching}
\label{subsec:controlled_ablations}
\label{subsec:factorial}
Before evaluating the untouched archive, we test which parts of the detector carry the live gain and which can be simplified without loss (Table~\ref{tab:ablations}):
\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*, nosep, itemsep=1pt]
    \item \emph{Position-plus-drift dials suffice at $M=3$:} On the fixed $19$-stream cache (Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(a)), expanding $1\to 224$ dials lifts causal Point-F1 from $0.013$ to $0.071$ ($p=0.0023$), and keeping only position and drift ($160$ dials) reaches $0.098$ at $0.55\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ (unweighted $N_{224}$ shifts archive PR-AUC by $\le 0.0011$). Under end-to-end \emph{Lite} retraining (Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(b)), adding a fourth prefix ($160$ vs.\ $128$ dials) gives no F1 or ER gain ($p=0.29$) while raising $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ ($0.82\%\to 1.06\%$) and slowing inference by $25.5\%$, matching the $118$-stream matched-rate rerun ($0.078$ vs.\ $0.073\text{--}0.074$ F1).
    \item \emph{Slimmer depth and parallel prediction hold up:} Under four-block retraining (Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(b)), four TCN blocks match five on F1 and ER ($p\ge 0.23$, saving $23.3\text{k}$ params), and $64$D heads lift causal ER over $128$D ($55.9\%\to 63.1\%$). Sequential rollout at $M=4$ drops buffered F1 ($0.239\to 0.195$) and doubles causal $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$ ($1.9\%\to 4.0\%$), whereas adding fluctuation and relay dials ($160\to 224$) trades higher causal ER ($63.6\%\to 69.0\%$) against higher $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ ($1.06\%\to 1.45\%$ causal; $6.8\%\to 11.9\%$ buffered).
    \item \emph{Whitening structure and fair PCA comparison:} Replacing dense $96{\times}96$ ZCA with horizon-preserving Kronecker ZCA ($\mathbf{I}_M\otimes\mathbf{\Omega}_D^{1/2}$) yields comparable archive accuracy rather than proven equivalence. Single-seed causal F1 ($s=42$) shifts by $+0.010$ (cluster CI $[-0.012,+0.027]$, $p=0.76$; ER $64.4\%$ vs.\ $62.9\%$), and three-seed F1 shifts by $+0.014$ (cluster CI $[-0.006,+0.029]$, $p=0.40$). Block-diagonal and dial ZCA match causal F1 ($0.095$ and $0.098$) while raising buffered ER by $+6.2$ and $+5.7$ points ($p\le 0.048$). Disentangling PCA eigenbasis rotation from (\ref{eq:stabilized_whitening}) index mismatch across three archive seeds clarifies the prior drop ($0.091\to 0.071$). Pure PCA matches pure ZCA on causal scores ($0.087$ vs.\ $0.086$ F1, $p=0.60$). Consistent eigenbasis shrinkage recovers the mismatched penalty ($0.086$ vs.\ $0.078$, stream CI $[+0.002,+0.014]$) and matches dense ZCA causally ($p=0.33$). Under buffered attribution, dense ZCA still leads consistent PCA ($0.166$ vs.\ $0.146$ F1, $p=0.005$), while Kronecker PCA matches Kronecker ZCA ($p\ge 0.68$).
    \item \emph{Weight-space quenching is redundant:} On a $118$-stream screen, predictor-row edits (Q1--Q4) leave causal F1 unchanged ($0.0901$ vs.\ $0.0904$) while perturbing unselected coordinates by $11.1\%$ RMS, so \emph{Lite} omits quenching.
    \item \emph{Hyperparameters plateau widely:} Retraining $D\in\{8,16,32,64\}$ beats Multi-Horizon 1D at every width ($56.1\%\text{--}61.0\%$ vs.\ $42.5\%\text{--}48.9\%$ ER), plateauing at $D\in\{16,32\}$ (PR-AUC $0.269$ at $D=32$; $D=8$ underfits buffered F1; $D=64$ dips to $0.068$ F1). Performance stays flat across $\lambda_{\text{damp}}\in[10^{-3},10^{-2}]$ ($0.080\text{--}0.093$ F1), $q_{\text{dial}}\in[0.975,0.990]$ ($0.080\text{--}0.090$), and $\kappa\in[1.00,1.20]$ ($\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}<1\%$ at $\kappa=1.12$).
\end{enumerate}

\noindent\emph{Why simpler coordinate rules fail (Table~\ref{tab:matched_alert}(a)):} On the 19-stream cache, thresholding $128$ coordinates without a quorum compounds errors into $40.6\%\text{--}66.5\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$, while \v{S}id\'{a}k ($0.5\%$) and BH FDR ($q=0.005$) collapse recall ($\text{F1}\le 0.001$) below the discrete $(N_{\text{cal}}+1)^{-1}$ floor. Matching $0.34\%$ nominal rate after aggregation avoids that floor, yet conformal $\min_j p_j$ jumps to $21.1\%$ $\text{FPR}_{\text{hold}}$ and Hotelling $T^2$, MEWMA, and CUSUM hit $27.9\%\text{--}58.6\%$, leaving the $160$-dial quorum ($0.098$ F1, $0.55\%$ FPR) and Fisher pooling ($0.109$ F1, $1.05\%$) ahead of top-$k$, $\max_j z_j$, and $\ell_2^2$ ($0.056\text{--}0.072$).

\subsection{Ablation 2: Sensor-Fault Taxonomy and Synthetic vs.\ Natural Failures}
\label{subsec:gating_ablation}
The latent panel flags \emph{cross-channel consistency violations}, \emph{abrupt multi-sensor transients}, and \emph{in-range phase shifts} because they perturb $>K_{\text{Lite}}$ coordinates simultaneously. Conversely, isolated single-sensor faults (freezing, variance collapse, saturation, quantization, dropouts, or single-channel bias) barely move the 32D summary: $K-1$ healthy channels dilute the input projection by $1/K$ and terminal LayerNorm rescales the remainder across all $32$ coordinates (on \texttt{SMAP P-3}, a flatline trips $12.5$ dials vs.\ $62.1$ for a harmonic flip).

An auxiliary channel screen across $19$ dev streams using $1.25\,q_{0.999}$, a $6.0$ median absolute deviation (MAD) floor, and $24$-of-$40$ persistence lifts recall on $57$ injected collapse, freezing, and saturation faults ($63.2\%\text{--}82.5\%\to 93.0\%\text{--}94.7\%$) and on shocks, ramps, and bias ($59.6\%\text{--}75.4\%\to 80.7\%\text{--}96.5\%$), but inflates clean $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$ from $1.5\%$ to $6.8\%\text{--}19.4\%$ and stays disabled.

\subsection{Ablation 3: Multiscale Front-End and Latent vs.\ Observation Space}
\label{subsec:dyadic_ablation}
\label{subsec:controlled_backbone}
Retraining with 4-shell rFFT splitting on five streams ($3$ seeds) lifts spacecraft F1 (\texttt{MSL M-1} $0.085\to 0.410$; \texttt{SMAP P-3} $0.212\to 0.321$) while lowering $K=9$ \texttt{Daphnet} and \texttt{GECCO} F1, making $K \ge 25$ a domain heuristic rather than causal proof. Across Table~\ref{tab:ablations}(c), observation reconstructors show heavy tails and drift ($\gamma_2 \approx 6.2$, $D_{\text{KS}} \approx 0.45$, $\text{FPR}\approx 25\%$). LayerNorm curbs kurtosis ($\gamma_2 = 0.91$) and latent whitening halves drift ($D_{\text{KS}} = 0.179\text{--}0.214$, $p \le 0.001$), while \emph{Lite} and 1D share kurtosis ($\gamma_2 = 2.33$ vs.\ $2.30$).

\subsection{Computational Complexity and Real-Time Deployment Considerations}
\label{subsec:computational_deployment}
Real-time inference stays compact in both memory and step latency. Canonical $32$D-head \emph{Lite} uses $120\text{k}\text{--}129\text{k}$ trainable parameters ($0.90\text{--}0.97\,\text{MB}$ FP32 with the target encoder; Table~\ref{tab:matched_alert}(b)). Across $K \in [5,72]$, the wider $64$D-head upper bound ($1.10\text{--}1.18\,\text{MB}$ FP32 including whitening buffers, plus $44\text{--}128\,\text{KB}$ state) runs in $0.16\text{--}0.25\,\text{ms}$/window on a batched GPU (RTX 5090) and $7.1\text{--}10.8\,\text{ms}$/window on one CPU thread ($93\text{--}141$ windows/s, $>2\times$ Daphnet's $64\,\text{Hz}$ rate). On $19$ dev streams with unannounced sensor dropouts, mean imputation retains $55.9\%\text{--}54.7\%$ ER (vs.\ $58.9\%$ clean) while $\text{FPR}_{\text{test}}$ rises from $1.5\%$ to $3.4\%\text{--}7.9\%$.
