\section{Revision Experiments}\label{sup:revision_experiments}

This section details the controlled revision experiments requested during review: the Experiment~A controlled score-conditioning study isolating geometry from representation dynamics, operating-characteristic analyses across nominal budgets and realized FPR, controlled-drift calibration stress tests, baseline cross-validation against published benchmarks, and inference-path ablation diagnostics.

\subsection{Controlled Score-Conditioning Study (Experiment A Full Contrast)}
To resolve whether low nominal false-positive rates originate from mechanical score boundedness or learned latent representation dynamics (Experiment~A), Table~\ref{tab:residual_diagnostics} provides a complete side-by-side comparison across 18 models and controls under identical 19-stream held-out SPOT calibration ($u=0.98, q=10^{-3}$, buffered regime).

Two distinct operational mechanisms are evident:
\begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Bounded score geometry compresses tail kurtosis ($\gamma_2$):} Imposing LayerNorm on low-dimensional projection bottlenecks mechanically prevents extreme residual excursions. The bounded observation control \texttt{TCN-ObsPred-LN} achieves $\gamma_2 = 0.14$ and \texttt{PCA-LinPred-LN} achieves $\gamma_2 = 0.87$ (matching or exceeding \texttt{TS-JEPA}'s $\gamma_2 = 1.14$), whereas unnormalized observation forecasting (\texttt{TCN-ObsPred}) and reconstruction (\texttt{TCN-ObsRecon}) exhibit severe excess kurtosis ($\gamma_2 = 8.24$ and $8.31$).
    \item \textbf{Learned representation dynamics are essential for out-of-sample threshold transfer and discriminative recall:} Kurtosis compression alone does not prevent threshold breakdown across temporal blocks. Under identical SPOT calibration, \texttt{TCN-ObsPred-LN} suffers an out-of-sample nominal FPR of $0.186$, matching \texttt{PCA-LinPred-LN} ($0.186$), compared to $0.080$ for \texttt{TS-JEPA} and $0.078$ for \texttt{Reynolds}. Training-free and linear controls collapse in downstream discriminative quality: \texttt{IsoForest} captures only $7.0\%$ of events (Point-F1 $0.041$), and \texttt{PCA-LinPred-LN} captures $32.0\%$ (Point-F1 $0.104$), compared to $47.3\%$ Event Recall (Point-F1 $0.234$) for \texttt{TS-JEPA}. Nonlinear latent representations are therefore strictly required to preserve threshold transfer and discriminative fault detection.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Multi-Dataset Baseline Benchmark Analysis}
Table~\ref{tab:sota_baselines} evaluates TS-JEPA against published deep anomaly detection baselines across all 11 shared benchmark datasets (130 continuous telemetry streams) using the unified out-of-sample calibration protocol (standard SPOT, $u=0.98, q=10^{-3}$). Two empirical findings stand out:

\emph{Contrastive sensitivity under tail calibration}: NCAD \cite{carmona2021neural}, which trains against synthetic point and contextual anomalies, achieves respectable Point-Adjusted F1 ($0.252$ macro, $0.298$ stream-level) but collapses to $0.058$ macro Point-F1 ($0.088$ stream-level). Point adjustment masks rampant false alarms because an isolated trigger within a long anomaly awards full segment credit. When evaluated strictly point-by-point, synthetic anomalies fail to transfer to physical process deviations (yielding near-zero detection on GHL, Genesis, and Metro). Similarly, dual-attention discrepancy contrasting in DCdetector \cite{yang2023dcdetector} and Anomaly Transformer \cite{xu2022anomaly} collapses to $0.070$ and $0.091$ macro Point-F1 due to severe tail-threshold overshoots on non-stationary baselines.

\emph{Forecasting versus latent representation}: Observation-space forecasters (TimesNet \cite{wu2023timesnet}: $0.129$ macro Point-F1; TranAD \cite{tuli2022tranad}: $0.156$) outperform contrastive baselines on point metrics but remain constrained by raw observation noise. TS-JEPA achieves leading macro Point-F1 ($0.160$) and stream-level Point-F1 ($0.186$), alongside leading Point-Adjusted F1 ($0.310$ macro, $0.401$ stream-level), indicating that predicting representations in latent space preserves fault dynamics across disparate physical domains without synthetic anomaly synthesis.
